Tale Forge icon sheet

Every icon and vector part harvested from tale-forge.app (37 icons, 4 endings-map parts, 10 glyph icons). Each icon is drawn at 24 px and 48 px on the surface it sits on in the product, using the site's own theme tokens (copied from source/css/3q17cp_jgfwol.pretty.css).
