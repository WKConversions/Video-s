# Globe template (from the Bingua globe ad, 46.5 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 6.1% motion)
A canvas dot globe (`src/globe.json` from `scripts/globe_dots.py`, orthographic, shaded) turned by a thumb, with stops
that light a country, drop a pin, raise a reel and fly a word chip home; a 3D ring of greetings; a plane on a dashed
route. Not included: footage and audio (`public/footage`, `public/audio`). Replace the brand in `lib.tsx`, the stops
and copy in `Story.tsx`.
