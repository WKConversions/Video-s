# Technique picker

Direction (Karl): 2D motion-design SaaS explainers: UI, meaning shapes, icons, kinetic type and charts. No realism
and no illustration or drawing styles; rows and techniques marked (illustration) are only for films that ask for them.

Start from what the beat must make the viewer understand, then take the technique whose meaning matches. These are
proven moves with measured timings, not decorations: if no row fits the meaning, invent; don't force one.

## Always, whichever technique

The rules at the end of the catalogue's section M, in short: accelerate into the cut and arrive moving; a scene turn about
every 2 s, none more than 5 s apart; breathe 8–14 drawn frames between ideas; blur on the peak frames only; anchor
one object through a turn; end cards keep moving; render and deliver at one frame rate; moving text on its own
layer while it moves; supersample the final render; score the hits a frame early and cut on the beat; state changes switch in one frame at a visible cause; anticipate big moves.
`scripts/qc.sh` measures most of them.
