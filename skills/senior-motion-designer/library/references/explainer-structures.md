# Explainer structures (measured)

Whole-film structures of motion-graphics SaaS explainers, mapped from start to end with their timings. Use at
steps 3-6 (script analysis, storytelling, visual strategy) to set the film's sections before choosing techniques
(`references/technique-picker.md`). Each entry is in Karl's Motion vault (folder "Explainer structure") with one
frame per second and a still per section.

## Walkway (Foundey), 49 s, pricing SaaS

| Section | Time | What it does | How it looks and moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem line | 0-2 s (4%) | names the pain in one line ("Pricing isn't easy") | kinetic type on brand violet; the pain words struck through with a hand-drawn scribble |
| Chapter 1 title | 2-6 s (8%) | the promise ("See the market" -> "Instantly compare prices") | two short lines, each replacing the last, violet field with diagonal slabs |
| Chapter 1 demo | 6-17 s (22%) | the feature working | real UI, always punched in; cursor clicks; comparison cards |
| Chapter 2 title | 17-19 s (4%) | the second promise | pull back from the UI, cut to the title landing big (M3) |
| Chapter 2 demo | 19-31 s (25%) | the second feature | tiles land, punch in, click and lift, whip to the result (M47); numbers (+EUR 345) |
| Confirmation | 32-36 s (8%) | the result happened ("Successfully Applied!") | the button becomes the scene, a progress line, then the success line on deep violet |
| Payoff | 37-41 s (10%) | the best feature last ("Auto Pilot") | a toggle switches on; the growth arrow draws the logo (M48) |
| End card + CTA | 42-49 s (14%) | brand, tagline, contact | mark, wordmark, tagline, URL; the opening scribble returns and loops (callback) |

Measured: UI on screen about 47%, type about 25%, brand and end about 28%. Rhythm: 9 big turns in 47 s, median gap
4.3 s, longest 11 s; relays median gap 8 drawn frames (46% tight). A narrated explainer turns about half as often as
an ad.

Style system: one brand colour for every type card; white for UI; a deeper shade for confirmation and payoff; one
hand-drawn accent used only at the start and the end (a callback); one sans serif, two short lines at most.

## AutoHDR (Obin Studio for Aryeo), 58 s, AI feature launch

| Section | Time | What it does | How it looks and moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin story | 0-7 s (12%) | trust: who built it and why | type on dark violet, ghosted UI behind |
| The gap | 8-13 s (10%) | what was missing ("Editing ... living outside the system") | UI windows scattered, drifting apart |
| The turn | 14-15 s (3%) | one word: "Now" | title lands on the outgoing scene's last frame (M8), then "You don't leave" types on |
| Hero button | 16-17 s (3%) | the feature is one click | extreme close-up of "Edit with AI", cursor press |
| Demos (3) | 18-52 s (60%) | choose, transform, custom edits | photo after photo transforms about 1 s each; a chapter line "Make any edit with AI" at 42 s |
| Co-branded end | 53-58 s (9%) | both brands | Aryeo mark + wordmark on dark, then AutoHDR on white |

Measured: 16 big turns in 57 s, median gap 2.3 s, longest 7.9 s; relays median gap 10 drawn frames (24% tight).
A feature launch turns almost twice as often as a feature explainer.

## Buildbox (Foundey), 64 s, product explainer on one continuous stage

| Section | Time | What it does | How it looks and moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-6 s (9%) | "See where users get stuck / Before they do." | kinetic type; dive through the "o" into the product (M31) |
| Problem shown | 6-11 s (8%) | users getting stuck | a cursor through the app, friction notes popping up |
| Steps 1-3 | 12-37 s (39%) | scan flows, generate agents, choose a flow | caption at the left (key phrase coloured), UI at the right transforming; a branded cursor |
| Findings | 38-47 s (16%) | the result | problem tags gather, turn red, become finding cards |
| Integration | 48-55 s (12%) | fits your tools | findings flow into ticket lists |
| Metrics | 56-57 s (3%) | proof | a grid of numbers with up-arrows |
| Logo + CTA | 58-66 s (12%) | "Book a demo at ..." | mark, wordmark, URL |

Measured: 1 big turn in 64 s (only the dive); relays median gap 8 drawn frames (42% tight). The one-stage register
replaces scene changes with transformations: rule 2 (a big change about every 2 s) applies to chaptered films and
ads, not to this register; here every 3-8 s the caption changes and the UI transforms instead.

## Sully (Motion the Agency), 23 s, AI agents product clip

| Section | Time | What it does | How it looks and moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pipeline | 0-3 s | the product at work | a dot becomes a note card; connector lines branch to agent cards on light beige |
| Claim | 3.5-5.5 s | "Most accurate AI Coder." | benchmark bars, the product lands first (M50) on deep blue |
| Extension | 6-8 s | "It doesn't stop at coding." | italic second line, cards cascading at the right |
| Feature toggle | 8.5-10 s | turn a capability on | a settings card, the cursor flips the toggle green |
| Agent chain | 10-20.5 s | three agents relay the work | Scribe -> Nurse -> Receptionist pills, status lines, relays (M4) |
| Next toggle | 21-23 s | the next capability | another settings card toggled on |

Measured: 8 big turns in 21 s, median gap 2.3 s, longest 4.9 s. Structure: claim with numbers, then "it doesn't
stop at X" and a chain of features, each opened by a toggle.

## Zocdoc (DemoDuck), the first 10 s as a hook (M80)

Task (1.5 s) -> pain scene (1.7 s) -> pain icons (1.7 s) -> the turn, a mascot badge (2 s) -> the name (2.3 s) ->
pull back to the product (about 0.8 s): six ideas in 10.5 s, about 1.75 s each, every pain one picture and one word.

## Narrated SaaS explainers: timing measured on 7 DemoDuck films (parallel round M18)

Problem / viewer's world until about 20-25% of the runtime (the product is named there, median 22%); product and
features about 65-70%, told as consequences for a person (often persona by persona); close 6-10% (4-9 s, median 5.5
s) with a callback to the opening line and the name or URL; one proof number at most, before the close. Story-led
and very short spots name the product later (38-52%).

## The template this gives (45-60 s SaaS explainer)

1. Problem line, 2 s, type only.
2. 2-3 chapters, each: title card 2-4 s, then demo 10-12 s (UI punched in, one click with weight, one number).
3. Confirmation of the result, 3-4 s, on its own field.
4. The payoff feature, 4-5 s, leading into the brand.
5. Logo, tagline and CTA, 6-7 s, with a callback to the opening and something still moving.
