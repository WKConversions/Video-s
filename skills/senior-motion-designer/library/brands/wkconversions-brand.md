# WKConversions brand

Use this whenever the video is for or about WKConversions. It replaces the skill's old bundled
`brands/wkconversions.md`, which only knew a dark `#03111d`. These values were taken from the live
wkconversions.com stylesheet on 28 September 2026. If you can reach the site, spot-check them; where
it has changed, the site wins.

The film's direction (`styles/README.md`) sets the look, for WKConversions' own videos too. Take the
colors, fonts, logo, founders and tone from here, and leave out the site look (the dot-grid page, the
white cards, the centered statement headlines).

**Company.** WKConversions (wkconversions.com): motion design and video editing for SaaS, e-commerce
and brands. The two co-founders, Raphael Wind and Karl van Kessel, design, animate and edit every
project themselves, and clients talk directly to them. Site headline: "Motion design that makes it
click."

**Color.**
- Page `#F7F7F8` with a dot grid (dots `#D6D9DE`). The page stays light throughout.
- Cards `#FFFFFF`, secondary surface `#EFF0F2`, borders `#E2E3E6`.
- Ink `#050F19`. Muted text `#575E68` and `#838A94`, faintest `#C9CED4`.
- Blue `#3F8CE8` (blue text on light backgrounds: `#1266C9`). Soft highlight `#E6F0FD`.
- Grey means noise and unread text. Blue marks what matters: the key phrase, the result, the CTA.

**Type.** Inter Tight 800 for headlines: tracking −0.05em, leading about 0.96, sentence case ending in
a period, with the last phrase in blue as in the site's h1. Inter 400–600 for body, UI and pills.
Tabular figures for counters. At 1080×1920 the test used 104–150 px headlines; minimum sizes are in
`design/visual-hierarchy.md`.

**Shapes.** The site's radii are 26 / 34 / 16 / 10 px plus full pills; scale them with the frame (the
test doubled them for a 1080-wide frame, e.g. 52 px cards). White rounded cards with a soft shadow
(`0 60px 128px -60px rgba(5,15,25,.40)` at 1080 wide). Blue filled eyebrow pills; white label pills
with a `#E2E3E6` border; a `#E6F0FD` highlight box behind the key word; founder portraits in circles
with a blue ring; the handwritten "wkc" mark, which works well as a stroke-by-stroke write-on. The
site hero's routed blue lines (6 px at 1080 wide, round caps, rounded corners, a node dot) are fine as
connectors or accents; using them to carry the story was one of the abstract devices cut after the
"no metaphors" feedback.

**Layout.** Centered statement headlines are part of the brand, the exception to "don't center by
default", but only for statements, not whole scenes. Demonstration content (paragraphs, UI, cards)
sits left-aligned or asymmetric for directional flow.

**Motion.** Clear, quick, settled: precise, controlled and confident, with deliberate scale and clean
transitions, and occasional cinematic emphasis. Arrivals use the site's own ease,
`cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`: a fast start and a long, soft settle (the other curves are in
`motion/easing.md`). No overshoot or bounce; motion blur, a slight 3D tilt and the constant camera
follow the motion craft. It should feel designed to move, not like things moving everywhere:
constant, but choreographed. Prefer one decisive transformation to five decorative animations.

**Impression.** Capable, modern, intentional, premium, performance-oriented, masculine without
becoming aggressive, confident without becoming loud.

**Avoid.** Gradient bloom and glow, sparkles, emoji and sticker aesthetics, strings of small centered
captions, decorative typewriter cursors, childish elastic motion, generic tech particles,
template-heavy corporate icon animation, and palette slams (the site is calm, and so is the motion).
