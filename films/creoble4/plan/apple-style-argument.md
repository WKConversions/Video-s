Here's the new Creoble film. Before you watch it, a short note on why we call it "Apple style". We didn't copy an Apple ad. We used the way Apple designs and animates its own products, and built it entirely in Creoble's brand: your sage and cream, Fraunces and DM Sans, your own wordmark and your real builder. Watch for these moments:

**0:00–0:02 — Nothing cuts, everything transforms.** "Din nye hjemmeside" springs in letter by letter. A small glass capsule bounces up with "få minutter", then stretches and becomes the browser window the site is built in. This is the core of Apple's motion language: one object turns into the next, so the eye never loses its place.

**0:03 — The Dynamic Island.** "Bygget af AI" drops in as the black capsule from the top of every new iPhone. It comes back as the film's status bar: "Betalt: 0 kr" (0:13), "Opdateret" (0:16), "Live · holmfysio.dk" (0:30) and "Creoble support · svarer nu" (0:41). At the end (0:51) it opens into your "Byg din side gratis" button. One recurring element tells the whole story, the way an Apple interface does.

**0:04–0:07 — Real physics.** The industry chips in your builder fall onto the form and bounce. The form flips over to reveal the finished site, which scrolls down and rubber-bands at the bottom, the way a page does on an iPhone. Apple's motion is built on springs: things have weight, momentum and a bounce, instead of sliding on a timer.

**0:14–0:16 — Liquid Glass.** The "Vil du ændre noget?" prompt bar is Liquid Glass, Apple's newest design material. It drops in, squashes as it lands and rounds out. The send button then travels on an arc into the site and melts into the new "Book tid" button. You don't just hear "Creoble retter det", you watch it happen.

**0:18–0:21 — Numbers and controls as heroes.** "499" rolls in like a counter and lands with a bounce. A single coin drops once for "Én gang". The "Abonnement" switch flips off: the exact iOS toggle, at a size where nobody can miss the point.

**0:23 — Restraint.** "Ikke tilfreds?" is the only 3D flip in the film. Apple uses its biggest moves once, at the turning point, which is why they land.

**0:28–0:39 — iOS notifications and a bento grid.** The setup steps stack in like iPhone notifications. Then everything you get for 83 kr a month builds into a bento grid, the tile layout Apple uses in every keynote to sum up a product: hosting, domain, e-mail, your name as the owner, servers in Europe, no lock-in. At 0:33 "Fast pris" expands to fill the grid, and at 0:34 the years roll forward while the price stays put.

**0:39–0:44 — Messages.** Support is shown as an iMessage-style conversation with the bouncing typing dots, and a human answer. The queue ticket pops like a bubble and the bot chip falls off the screen.

**0:44–0:50 — Apple keynote typography.** "Creoble." lands with its full stop first. Two glass tiles, website and hosting, slide together and merge into one card exactly on "samlet ét sted". Then "Byg den. Se den. Så køber du." in big serif type on black: one statement at a time, like a keynote slide.

**Throughout.** The film runs at 60 frames per second, so every move is fluid, and something meaningful is always moving with the voice-over without the screen ever getting busy. There are never more than a few things on screen. Everything uses your colours, your type and your interface.

In short, it uses Apple's design language with Creoble's voice and brand. I'm curious what you think.
