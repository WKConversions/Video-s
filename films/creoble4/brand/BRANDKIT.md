# Creoble brand kit (from the client, 21 Oct 2026)

This is the official Creoble brand. It overrides the tokens the v1–v3 films used (see "What changes" below).

## Colours
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Sage (primary) | #87A89E |
| Light sage | #A8C4BB |
| Background | #FAFAF8 |
| Text | #1A1614 |

## Wordmark: "Creoble."
- Files:
  - `creoble-wordmark-dark.svg`: for light grounds. Text #1A1614, full stop in sage #87A89E.
  - `creoble-wordmark-light.svg`: for dark grounds. Text #F5F1EA, full stop in light sage #A8C4BB.
  - Both are outlined (no font needed) and have the same geometry (viewBox 0 0 7208 1511).
- Type (from Creoble's code):
  - Fraunces Regular, roman (not italic), weight 400;
  - optical size 24;
  - letter-spacing −0.025em.

## Type
- **Fraunces 400** for the wordmark (as above).
- **DM Sans** for body text, navigation and buttons:
  - Regular 400 for body and navigation;
  - Medium 500 for buttons;
  - small labels in Medium 500, uppercase, with wide letter-spacing.

The client's wishes still stand:
- cream or off-white background;
- the brand colour as the primary colour, with cream as the secondary;
- no orange;
- a clean, minimalist, Apple-like look.
