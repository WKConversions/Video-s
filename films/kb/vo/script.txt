KB Network Intelligence is a system that turns everyday information into real business opportunities. Here's how it works. First, conversations, meetings, emails, and documents are collected. Claude processes this information into structured summaries. Once approved, everything is stored in a central knowledge base, connected to KB's client ecosystem. AI then analyzes this knowledge alongside external data, identifying potential partnerships, referrals, investments, and opportunities within existing clients. Specialized agents develop these opportunities into actionable proposals. Management reviews each stage before personalized outreach is prepared, approved, sent, and tracked. For example, when a client mentions launching a new business, the system can recognize potential needs, identify relevant connections, and suggest the next steps. Every outcome feeds back into the knowledge base, continuously improving the information available for future decisions.
