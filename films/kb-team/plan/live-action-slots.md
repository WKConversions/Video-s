# Live action in the K.B team film: the slots to fill

The reference cuts filmed shots into its motion design: warm, lived-in rooms, natural window light, shallow depth of
field, real people absorbed in their work, a calm camera. Nothing looks like corporate stock (no suits shaking hands,
no white offices, no people grinning at the lens). The K.B film needs the same kind of shots, with K.B's meaning.
Footage must be royalty-free for commercial use with no credit required: **Mixkit** (the skill's `stock.py` searches
it). Each slot lists the reference frames to match and what the K.B shot must say.

Rules for every clip:
- Each of AA–AD overlays one line of small white text in the centre, and AN larger white words: the middle of the frame
  must be calm (no faces or bright windows right behind the text).
- The four AA–AD shots must look like four different people in four different places, with a common warmth.
- No visible brand logos, no readable screens with other companies' products, no children, no medical or political
  settings. People's faces may be visible; nobody looks into the lens.
- At least 1920×1080, 24 fps or more, no cuts inside the part we use, at least twice the slot's length of steady
  footage, little or no camera shake. A slow push or drift is welcome; a static camera is fine.
- Colour: warm and natural, or gradeable to it. Avoid heavy teal-orange looks, blown highlights and noisy low light.
