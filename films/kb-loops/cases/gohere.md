# GoHere: K.B case loop

Source: https://kruslockbosconsultancy.com/client-cases/gohere (fetched 12 Nov 2026). Only these facts go on screen.

## Facts (case page)
- **Title:** "A Complete Website Rebuild for GoHere in Just Two Days". GoHere helps destinations connect with visitors
  through custom digital applications. K.B rebuilt their website in two days: a fresh design, a content management system
  and a stronger foundation for online visibility.
- **Challenge:** GoHere's own website wasn't reflecting the quality of their product. It needed a clearer structure, a
  more modern design and better visibility in search engines. Managing content was unnecessarily complicated: the team
  couldn't easily make updates themselves.
- **K.B's approach:** three things in one short sprint: a website redesign (fresh visual design, clearer structure); a
  CMS so the team can edit pages and publish content without a developer; SEO and GEO optimisation (content, structure
  and metadata for traditional search engines and AI-powered search platforms).
- **Results:** 2 Days (complete website rebuild); 1 CMS (independent content management); 2 Search Channels (SEO and
  GEO). Quote: "Fast, reliable and precise. Very happy with the delivery and process." (GoHere)

## Loop
Concept: "Two days, two wipes". A single browser window (toolbar with "gohere.app") holds GoHere's old site: grey,
crooked, cluttered blocks that bob out of line, the product shown tiny and greyed in a corner, a padlock on the content
(the cursor clicks it and it refuses) and a search badge that shakes "nothing found". A K.B calendar chip on the right
counts the days. The K.B tile drops onto the window's left edge and becomes a squeegee blade: Day 1 it wipes left to
right and the new GoHere site appears behind it (logo, navy headline, navy and mint pills, the app phone on a mint/sky
card, the navy logo band); the calendar flips 1 → 2; Day 2 it wipes back right to left and leaves the CMS behind
(dashed edit outlines, mint pencil badges) and a search card with its two channels (search + AI). The calendar chip then
grows into a big calendar page: "2 days". It shrinks back, the blocks tilt and grey back into the old site one by one,
and the tile lifts away: the opening again.

Flourish: "the squeegee". The K.B tile stretches into a full-height blade that leans into its own speed and wipes the
window; the old site smears sideways in its wake (a horizontal motion smear that thins and dissolves into the new
design, with a cyan wet edge), so every old block leaves a streak that turns into the next state.
