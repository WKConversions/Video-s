# JongLeren: K.B case loop

Source: https://kruslockbosconsultancy.com/client-cases/jongleren (fetched 12 Nov 2026). Only these facts go on screen.

## Facts (case page)
- **Title:** "Automating Candidate Intake for Faster Placements". By automating intake and admin work, JongLeren freed
  recruiters to focus on matching the right students with the right internships.
- **Challenge:** the intake process was draining recruiter time and slowing down placements. Each candidate journey needed
  multiple touchpoints, a 40-minute intake call and another 30 minutes of manual CRM updates. Recruiters often found out
  halfway through that a candidate wasn't even eligible: frustration, lost time, inconsistent data.
- **K.B's approach:** redesigned the intake workflow end to end: a process audit; automation (online candidate forms with
  instant eligibility feedback, AI transcription of intake calls, CRM auto-updates, auto-generated candidate PDFs);
  training and onboarding; continuous optimisation. The page shows the Make scenario behind it
  (`public/cases/jongleren/intake-automation-ref.jpg`).
- **Results:** ~40h time saved per month (each intake cut from 70 minutes to 20-30 minutes); 90% error reduction;
  ~€1,200 cost impact per month; 100% team impact. Quote: "We are very impressed with the outcomes of the intake calls."
  (Marisa, JongLeren).

## Loop
Concept: "Stop juggling the intake". A single centre-left stage: JongLeren's white intake card (red JL monogram and
wordmark in its header, a red stopwatch, four empty dashed rows) with two navy "hands" above it juggling four icon balls in
a shower pattern: the intake call, the CRM sheet, the notes, and a "?" for eligibility. The text sits in a right-hand
column. The "?" ball is thrown wild, turns red halfway up and falls past the card; the K.B tile pops up and catches it,
flips it to a cyan check and tosses it into the first row, then docks as a sticker on the card's corner. The other balls
are pulled out of the air one by one into the rows (Transcript, Candidate PDF, CRM updated), the hands fold away and the
stopwatch shrinks from a long red arc to a short cyan one. "~40h saved per month" counts up. The rows gather back into
balls that spring up into the juggle, the K.B sticker pops off: the opening again.
Flourish: the splat. Each ball is plucked mid-flight, stretches into a cyan comet with a fading ghost trail, hits its
row, squashes flat and spreads sideways into the filled row bar with a ripple (the ball becomes the row); at the end the
row gathers back into a ball and springs up out of it.
