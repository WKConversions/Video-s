# Abroad Internships: K.B case loop

Source: https://kruslockbosconsultancy.com/client-cases/abroad-internships (fetched 12 Nov 2026). Only these facts go on
screen.

## Facts (case page)
- **Title:** "Scaling Operations With 21 Automations". Abroad Internships proved that automation enables sustainable
  growth: fewer people, fewer errors, a better candidate experience.
- **Challenge:** Abroad Internships managed internships and outreach with a team of 7 people, heavily relying on manual
  work: candidate follow-ups, payment confirmations, company outreach and CRM updates. Inefficient, expensive, hard to
  scale.
- **K.B's approach:** process audit; automation (online candidate forms with instant eligibility feedback, AI
  transcription of intake calls, CRM auto-updates, auto-generated candidate PDFs); training and onboarding; continuous
  optimisation. The case page shows the scenario K.B built: rows of round modules (`automation-ref.png`).
- **Results:** ~30h time saved per week (120+ hours per month); 200% (candidate satisfaction up among rejected
  applicants); 150% (CRM costs, ~€6,000 per year saved); 100% team impact (7x fewer employees while increasing revenue);
  +20% best-performing month. Quote: "The automation they've introduced strikes a perfect balance between efficiency and
  personalization…" (Susanne, Founder).

## Loop
Concept: "The week drains into K.B". One big centred planner card: Abroad Internships' working week (logo in the
header, a stack of 7 team avatars, M–F columns), crammed with 21 manual task blocks in their colours (follow-ups indigo,
payments orange, outreach lilac, CRM sand) that keep landing until the last ones pile crooked on top of the full week.
The K.B tile drops into the middle of the week and shoves the blocks aside; a ring of 21 grey dots opens round it. In a
clockwise radar sweep every block takes off and spirals into the tile; each one that lands lights one dot (21
automations). The planner is left empty, a cyan wash of free time spreads from the tile, the tile glides left and
"~30h saved per week" settles beside it. The figure folds away, the tile pops out and the tasks land again: the opening.
Flourish: "the flight-path vortex". Each block squashes, rounds into a ball and whips into the tile as a tapered swoosh
in its own colour with a chevron head (the orange arc arrow of the Abroad Internships logo), leaving a dashed
airline-route trail that drains in after it, so the whole week visibly swirls down into K.B like a route map.

Layout: headline centred above the card; planner card centred; K.B tile at the centre of the week (later left of the
figure); a Barcelona stamp tucked on the card's top-right corner.
