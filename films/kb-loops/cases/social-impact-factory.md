# Social Impact Factory: K.B case loop

Source: https://kruslockbosconsultancy.com/client-cases/social-impact-factory (fetched 12 Nov 2026). Only these facts go on
screen.

## Facts (case page)
- **Title:** "Connecting Booking and CRM Data for a Complete Customer Journey". By integrating AQQO with Pipedrive, Social
  Impact Factory gained a centralized view of customer activity, booking history and lead sources, without manually
  transferring information between systems.
- **Challenge:** Social Impact Factory managed coworking spaces, meeting rooms, memberships and event bookings through
  AQQO while implementing Pipedrive as its new CRM. The two platforms operated independently, so customer and booking
  data remained fragmented.
- **K.B's approach:** an API-based integration between AQQO and Pipedrive: integration architecture (the complete data
  flow), API integration, Make.com workflows that retrieve, process and synchronize booking and customer data, data
  mapping (booking details, customer information and booking sources to the correct Pipedrive fields), record management
  (new CRM records created or existing ones updated automatically), a scalable foundation for more automations.
- **Results:** 100% automated synchronization ("systems remained synchronized at all times"); 1 dashboard overview.
  Quote: "We are highly satisfied with the communication, delivery speed, and final solution." (Silvie).
- **Built with:** Make, Pipedrive, AQQO (all three in `public/img/tools`).

## Loop
Concept: "The trail becomes the bridge". A symmetric two-island layout: SIF's AQQO booking card (dark SIF header with
the logo, rows for meeting room, coworking, membership, event, each "Booked") on the left, the Pipedrive contacts card
(empty dashed fields with "?") on the right, a broken grey dashed link between them. Bookings try to cross and bounce
off the break. The K.B tile drops into the gap at the top of the arch; the top booking squashes and shoots across, and
its smear lays a permanent cyan cable through the K.B tile (Make badge pops on). Bookings then ride the cable one by one
and morph into the CRM fields. "100%" grows down out of the K.B tile under the arch, framed by SIF's yellow brackets.
The cable splits at the top and retracts into the two dashed stubs; new empty contacts scroll in: the opening again.
Flourish: the first booking smears across the gap as a tapered cyan comet, and its wet trail thins and stays as the
cable itself (the smear becomes the infrastructure); at the end the cable snaps at the apex and recoils into the stubs.
