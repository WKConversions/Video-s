# Göteborgsvarvet: K.B case loop

Source: https://kruslockbosconsultancy.com/client-cases/goteborgsvarvet (fetched 12 Nov 2026). Only these facts go on screen.

## Facts (case page)
- **Client:** Göteborgsvarvet, the Gothenburg half marathon ("one of the world's largest half marathon events").
- **Challenge:** collecting CO2-related data for sustainability reporting across the whole event ecosystem (internal staff,
  event personnel, suppliers, sponsors, partners, collaborators) in areas such as travel, food, materials, logistics and
  other emissions. The process ran on complex Excel files, manual follow-ups and scattered submissions.
- **K.B's approach:** a custom web-based CO2 data collection platform: a public submission portal (no account needed,
  organisation picked from a dropdown, structured forms per category: travel, food, materials, other emissions),
  centralised storage, an admin dashboard (partner and submission tracking, manual records, filtering and reporting
  overview) and an Excel export aligned with the existing CO2 model.
- **Results:** 100% successful submissions rate; 10/10 client satisfaction. Quote: "What felt like an impossible challenge
  was made very easy to understand and complete." (Elin Bondemark, Project Manager).

## Loop concept: "Every lane to one finish"
Concept: four scattered spreadsheet files (travel, food, materials, other emissions; GV colours) float around while a
follow-up envelope hops from file to file and drops red badges; the K.B tile arrives at the centre, the files line up as
runners in a left start grid, the tile grows into a white GV dashboard card on the right, and four lanes converge into one
finish straight that feeds a donut ring on the card. Each file runs its lane into the ring, which fills lap by lap to
100%. The card folds back into the K.B tile and the files burst out of it to their scattered spots (the opening).
Flourish: each file squashes into a pill and smears into a long GV-coloured stripe with a white inline down its middle
(the letter style of the GV logo), leaving a painted lane and a fading ghost tail; it laps the ring over the segments
already filled and settles as its own quarter of the donut. After 100% a white glint runs one victory lap.

Layout: headline top-left; start grid of files on the left; four converging lanes in the middle; dashboard card on the
right (GV logo in its header, donut, "successful submissions" label).
