# Vino Mio: K.B case loop

Source: https://kruslockbosconsultancy.com/client-cases/vino-mio (fetched 12 Nov 2026). Only these facts go on screen.

## Facts (case page)
- **Title:** "Saving Vino Mio 30 Hours Every Month Through Booking Automation". Vino Mio is a restaurant in the heart of
  Málaga, known for its Mediterranean cuisine and live flamenco dinner shows. K.B connected their booking platforms,
  removing manual coordination.
- **Challenge:** many guests book the flamenco dinner through GetYourGuide, but tables and availability live in Zenchef.
  The two platforms weren't connected, so every booking required manual attention: reservations arrived by email or
  WhatsApp, the team matched each booking with an available table, confirmed arrival times and kept both systems
  aligned. This took senior management time every day.
- **K.B's approach:** connected GetYourGuide with Zenchef; live availability synchronisation between both platforms
  (guests can only book what the restaurant can accommodate); reservations made through GetYourGuide flow directly into
  Zenchef.
- **Results:** 30 hours / month management time saved; 1 hour / day less daily administration; 2 platforms fully
  connected. Quote: "The team at K.B is always on top of things, available and helpful whenever necessary." (Freddy
  Cusell, Vino Mio).

## Loop
Concept: "Every booking finds its table". A top-down floor plan of the Vino Mio dining room fills the right of the
frame as one wide white Zenchef card (Vino Mio seal and Zenchef tile in its header): six round tables with chairs and a
pale-coral flamenco stage with a guitar on the right wall. On the left, under the GetYourGuide tile, booking messages
(envelopes and green chat bubbles) keep dropping into a crooked pile, and a navy manager cursor with a red "time" ring
drags one booking by hand to a table (a dashed pending ring), then has to go back up and tap GetYourGuide to keep it in
step. The K.B tile drops onto the card's left edge; the pile flips into neat GetYourGuide tickets, a 3×2 live
availability mini-map pops up beside the GetYourGuide tile, and each ticket slides into K.B and is poured straight into a
table, which fills Vino Mio blue and gets its coral ring (the table becomes the Vino Mio seal) while its dot on the
mini-map fills in sync (the ticket queue moves up a slot each time). A cyan ripple runs over the full room, K.B pulses a sync
heartbeat, the flamenco stage comes alive (the guitar strums, coral notes rise) and "30h saved per month" counts up below. The tables drain,
the mini-map and K.B leave, and the messages start dropping again: the opening.
Flourish: "the pour". Out of the K.B tile each booking jets as a glossy liquid ribbon (thick head, thinning tail) that
arcs over the room like wine poured from a bottle and splashes into its table; the ribbon drains into the table, which
fills from the bottom with a sloshing, settling surface. At the end the tables drain back the same way.
