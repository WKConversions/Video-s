# Recruiting Rewards: two SaaS explainers (no voice-over), 40 s each, 1920×1080, 30 fps

On-screen words are short Lora headlines in the site's voice (one phrase in their green), the product is their real
interface rebuilt from the demo films, the music carries the pace and UI sounds land on every interaction. Facts and
copy: `../harvest/facts.md`. Style: the site's warm off-white page (#F8F6F5), stone ink (#1C1917), deep green
(#287E61) pills, hairline borders (#E2DFD9), amber accent dot (#E0A458), navy CTA panel (#0B1F3A); Lora 600
headlines, Manrope UI. Motion: confident and clean: one move at a time, a fast start and a long soft settle, the
camera breathing, transitions that grow out of the card or button on screen.

Thread object in both films: the assignment card. For recruiters it is picked, filled with a candidate and turns into
a reward; for companies it is written from one sentence, published, worked by recruiters and turns into a hire.
