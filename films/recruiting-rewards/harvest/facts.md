# Recruiting Rewards: facts the films may use

Source: recruitingrewards.com (home, /recruiters, /companies, harvested 15 October 2026) and the two product demo films
on those pages (`assets/demo/recruiter.mp4`, `company.mp4`). Every line on screen comes from here.

## The business
- "The AI hiring marketplace" (demo title card). Pay-per-hire hiring marketplace (page title).
- "The right skills. The right assignment. The right match." / "Post a role for free, pay when the candidate starts the job." (footer)
- "Recruiting Rewards. Pay for results." (company demo end card)
- Core market: the Nordics: Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland.

## For companies (/companies)
- "No cost until someone is hired." "Post the role you are looking for at no cost. Recruiting Rewards matches it with
  recruiters who have the right skills, industry knowledge and network. You pay when the candidate starts the job."
- Banner: "Companies cut hiring costs by 30-45% with Recruiting Rewards."
- Why it works: Free to post, pay only on result ("No retainer, no monthly fee, no minimum volume."); 30 to 45% lower
  cost than a traditional agency; Qualified candidates: a recruiter does the screening, not an algorithm; Faster
  process: niched recruiters with the right network from day one.
- How it works: 1 Post assignment · 2 Get matched ("One assignment, several recruiters, the right expertise.
  Specialists in your industry work the role in parallel, each with their own network.") · 3 Collaborate ("Automatic
  workflows, shared workspaces, and direct messaging with recruiters.") · 4 Reward ("Only pay when you find the right
  hire. The recruiter who delivers gets rewarded.")
- Stats on the page: 100+ candidates hired via the platform; 94+ companies; 175+ specialist recruiters.
- CTA: "Start hiring".
- Product (demo): "Who do you want to hire today?" one sentence, "6/7 criteria detected" with criteria chips (Role name,
  Seniority, Education, Location, Industry, Skills, Company); Aifos writes the ad ("A full draft in seconds"); Publishing
  and reward ("SEK 95 000 reward per hired", "The reward is calculated from the salary"); Response time to candidates
  (2/3/4 business days); Questions for recruiters; Send for verification; Candidate pipeline (Waiting, In process,
  Interview 1…); candidate profile (AI score, rating, recruiter, recruiter motivation); "Congrats, Oskar Vinge is hired!"
  with the invoice breakdown ("The reward is invoiced … when the candidate starts the job.").

## For recruiters (/recruiters)
- "Stop selling. Start recruiting." "Get access to real recruitment assignments without spending your time on cold
  calls, meeting booking and sales. Pick the assignments that fit your expertise and focus on what you do best:
  finding and qualifying the right candidate."
- "200+ specialist recruiters already working here"; "Over 200 specialist recruiters work assignments through
  Recruiting Rewards."
- Why it works: Skip the sales work: the assignments are already on the platform ("No cold calls, no pitch decks, no
  negotiating terms with a new client. Companies publish the brief and the reward, and you decide what to take on.");
  More time for sourcing, screening and candidate relationships; Access to more assignments than you can source on
  your own; Structured briefs, requirement profiles and payment terms.
- How it works: 1 Pick assignments · 2 Submit candidates ("Present screened candidates with your own written
  motivation. The company reads your assessment next to the CV.") · 3 Collaborate ("Interview feedback, messages and
  status changes stay in one thread") · 4 Get rewarded ("When your candidate starts the job, the reward is invoiced and
  paid out on the terms you saw before you accepted the assignment.")
- CTA: "Join as a recruiter".
- Product (demo): Open Jobs list (company, role, positions, location, guarantee, HIRED / IN PROCESS / WAITING, "SEK
  140,000 REWARD PER HIRED", "Recommend"); "A hundred open assignments from companies that pay for results"; AI Match
  Analysis ("Meets the quality threshold (9/7)", score 9, Domain and language fit 9/10, Relevant senior experience
  9/10, Strong technical alignment 9.5/10, key signals); "A clear score and a clear why. Below the threshold, it cannot
  be sent."; "Submitted. The company sees her in their pipeline within seconds."
