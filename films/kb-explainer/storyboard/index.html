Project direction

PurposeA product-explainer for K.B's website and LinkedIn: what K.B does, how it works, and the call to talk.
AudienceFounders and managers of startups and SMEs whose work still runs on manual steps.
Format16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 58.0 s.
Core messageK.B builds the software and automation your business runs on, with you: diagnose, build, run, one loop.
ReferenceThe JustCall × HubSpot explainer: logo circles open into an app window, a module switches on, a phone slides in, "How?", one window whose body changes while cards lift out of it, a dropdown pick, a workflow, tools joined up, avatars, a month calendar, an infinity loop, a logo-and-button end card, a brand mark in the corner. Nothing of its artwork, screens or words is used.
Style and brandK.B's site: canvas #F7F7F7 with soft blobs, white rounded windows with a navy app bar, cyan as the action colour (never text on light), Montserrat; Copperplate only in the K.B logotype.
MotionPrecise and calm: arrivals on a gentle curve, MOVE for things already on screen, no overshoot. Windows stay whole in the frame and only creep (≤ 3% a second); cards lift out of them for emphasis, as in the reference. The camera only breathes.
Words on screenOnly "How?" and "Need more?" as big words (as in the reference); everything else is interface text inside the pictures.
SoundElevenLabs voice (Christina) at 1.60 s; Mixkit "Tears of Joy" cut on its bar grid, the band coming in as the audit window rises after "How?", the song's last hit on "K.B" and the end card; 139 effects on their moves (clicks, typing, landings, chimes).

Notes

  • The voice-over is the script Karl recorded (script.md); nothing was rewritten. "K.B" is spoken and shown; "Consultancy" never appears.
  • Remade after the reference's structure, pacing and kinds of motion; none of its screens, logos, illustrations or wording is used. Every interface is an example built from K.B's own services and words.
  • People are initials only (AM, JS, RK, LT, You); K.B is its logo tile. Jordan Smith and Acme Studio are placeholder names in the example form; the dashboard numbers are labelled "Example data".
  • Tool logos (Pipedrive, Odoo, Supabase, Resend, Make, n8n, Claude, Next.js) are the ones K.B's site shows as tools it builds with.
  • No URL on the end card (the domain contains "consultancy"); the button is the site's own "Book a Free Discovery Call".
  • Nothing from the site's hero video (or any of its videos) was downloaded, sampled or used.
