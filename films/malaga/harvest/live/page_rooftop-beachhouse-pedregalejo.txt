### About this property
Light and spacious inside, with a ground-floor bathroom and another bathroom on the first floor. In total there are four bedrooms, sleeping up to eight people.
The rooftop is the showpiece: it feels like an extra spacious living room, with a wonderful view and a BBQ that you can use freely.
Up for the beach? The lovely beach of Pedregalejo is literally just around the corner, with great restaurants and bars.
### In short
Maximum of 8 people
4 bedrooms
2 bathrooms, on the ground floor and on the first floor
Rooftop with barbecue and views
Pedregalejo beach just around the corner
