# Casa Málaga Beach
## Casa Málaga Beach
#### in Pedregalejo - Málaga Beach
#### This fantastic apartment is literally around the corner from the beach in the charming village of Pedregalejo.
#### Fantastic flat for 4 people
Dream of staying somewhere where you can smell the sea as soon as you open the door? Then this apartment in Pedregalejo is exactly what you're looking for!
The apartment sleeps a maximum of 4 people, is in a prime location, and is just a stone's throw from the beach. This is the ideal base for anyone who wants to experience Malaga in a truly local way.
You can enjoy all the charm that Pedregalejo has to offer here. And believe us: this fishing village is truly unique.
Narrow streets, colourful fishermen's cottages, cosy beach bars, and espetos (grilled sardines) straight from the beach.
And if you want to seek out the hustle and bustle of Málaga's vibrant centre, you'll be there in about 20 minutes, all easily doable by bus.
Is this an authentic neighbourhood so close to a big city? Fantastic outdoor cafés literally around the corner, but you do experience the peace where the apartment is located. This stay is so local and so cosy, you'll hardly find that anywhere else!
