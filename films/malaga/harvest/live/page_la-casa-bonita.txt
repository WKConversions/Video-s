## La Casa Bonita
#### Stylish 4-person holiday home in El Palo, Málaga, with unique plunge pool
Welcome to La Casa Bonita, a charming and luxurious holiday home where you immediately get a taste of Spanish life. The house is located in the quiet, authentic neighbourhood of El Palo, known for its cosy tapas bars and local atmosphere. Yet you are in the bustling centre of Málaga within 20-30 minutes by bus.
The holiday home is fully equipped for a carefree stay and exudes a relaxed beachy vibe.
Discretion and quality are paramount, making La Casa Bonita the place for guests who appreciate exclusivity and comfort.
Guest favouriteon AirbnbSuperhosthost on Airbnb for 2 years
La Casa Bonita features:
A master bedroom with a luxurious double bed and ensuite bathroom
A second bedroom with two single luxury beds and a separate bathroom
Spacious living room with large open-plan kitchen
Large sunny terrace and garden with outdoor shower, 4 comfortable loungers and a fine spot for breakfast and dinner
Covered outdoor kitchen with fridge
A plunge pool - this is a rare luxury in El Palo, perfect for cooling off and relaxing
The location is ideal: you stay in the middle of an authentic Spanish neighbourhood with cosy bars and eateries around the corner, while you can easily explore the city of Málaga.
Whether you come for sun, sea and beach, a city break or just to enjoy the real Spanish life: La Casa Bonita offers you comfort, luxury and an authentic experience.
The managers will personally welcome you and take care of the check-in at the holiday home.
