# Happy House
## Happy House
#### Suitable for up to 6 people
Casa Feliz (or “the happy house”) is our newest addition in the charming El Palo and one of the most authentic neighbourhoods of Málaga.
And believe us: you don't want to miss this.
Spacious home spread over three floors
Spacious eat-in kitchen & cosy living room on the ground floor
Two bedrooms (1 bedroom with 2 single beds and 1 bedroom with a double bed) on the first floor, with a bathroom
Third bedroom (with a double bed) on the second floor with its own bathroom and an adjoining terrace with an outdoor kitchen
On the top floor, a delightful roof terrace with sun loungers, perfect for dining together, relaxing, and enjoying Spanish life.
All of this within walking distance of the beach and right in the heart of the Spanish locals.
Casa Feliz. As the name suggests: it just makes you happy here.
