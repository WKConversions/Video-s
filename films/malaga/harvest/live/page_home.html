Frequently asked questions

a

Do you offer help in booking flights or putting together a trip/team building trip?

Yes, we also support you in booking flights and organising your entire trip. Should there be interest in a team-building trip, we can also put this together and take care of it.

a

How do I stay informed about what is happening to my property?

We keep you regularly informed through detailed reports, photos and updates. You always have access to the rental calendar and can contact us directly if you have any questions. Transparency and communication are essential to us.

a

How often will my property be cleaned?

The house is thoroughly cleaned after each stay. Depending on the number of rental periods and your requirements, we can also arrange cleaning in between (for longer stays).

a

How does rental during high and low season work?

Lettings are seasonal, with peak summer and holiday periods. We advise on optimal rental and pricing strategies and adjust marketing accordingly to maximise your occupancy rates.

a

How is my property secured and monitored when I am not there?

Many owners already choose an appropriate security strategy when buying their property. We do not actively get involved in this, but through our network we can put you in touch with reliable parties for alarm systems, smart locks or camera surveillance, for example. During renting out, we check whether everything is running as it should and inform you immediately of any peculiarities.

a

How do you ensure good reviews and satisfied guests?

We value a positive guest experience. We ensure a professional welcome (in person if necessary), a clean and well-kept property, and clear communication before, during and after the stay. In addition, we like to give local tips and there is an information folder (+ online guest link app, with tips and information) from us in the residence and we are on standby in case of questions or problems. Satisfied guests are more likely to leave a good review and book again.

a

How does Málaga Hideaway ensure that my property is always in perfect condition?

We treat your property as if it were our own. This means we do periodic inspections to make sure everything is working properly. If repairs are needed, we arrange them quickly and efficiently with reliable professionals. In addition, we make sure the property is always clean and ready for guests or your own stay.

a

Can I register my property with Málaga Hideaway so that I get bookings myself?

You can do that too! You can register your property via our website, this service is free of charge. In case of a successful booking via our site, we charge an interesting commission. All communication and further handling goes through the landlords/hosts of the accommodation themselves.

a

Can I use my property myself whenever I want?

Yes, you always retain the right to use your property yourself. We share a booking calendar and then you can see for yourself when days are free to block them off privately.

a

Can I book directly with Málaga Hideaway?

Yes, you can book directly with us, this saves and is always cheaper than the same accommodation we have on the booking platforms.

a

What happens if damage occurs during rental?

In case of damage, we will report it to you immediately. We will help recover the damage through the insurance company.

a

What is the rental management process at Málaga Hideaway and what does it involve?

We take over the complete rental management of your property in Malaga. This means we take care of:

  • Professional photos and online ads
  • Management of bookings and communication with guests
  • Welcoming guests (in person if necessary)
  • we schedule cleaning and provide clean bed linen and towels at every change.
  • We do periodic checks and see if any maintenance is needed. If so, we report this to you in a timely manner.

You don't have to worry about anything yourself; we take care of everything down to the last detail.

a

What makes Málaga Hideaway different from other managers?

We offer a personal, transparent and committed service with great attention to your home and your needs. If required, we can schedule a video call to discuss progress.

a

What kind of maintenance can Málaga Hideaway provide for my property?

We provide both minor maintenance, such as lamp replacement. Major maintenance, such as renovations are by mutual agreement.

a

What are the costs and what will I keep from the rental?

When you fill in the enquiry form via our website, we will provide you with a tailor-made quote.

a

What local rules and permits do I need to know for holiday rentals in Málaga?

The rules around holiday rentals in Spain vary by region and change regularly. Therefore, we always recommend checking up-to-date information with a lawyer or local specialist.

We have good contacts who can help with this and are also happy to think along with you based on our experience. For official legal advice, however, we always refer you to an expert.
