# La Perla Escondida
## La Perla Escondida
#### in Alhaurín de la Torre
#### Book this hidden gem on the Costa del Sol
Welcome to La Perla Escondida, a fantastically spacious villa for up to 8 people, where peace, space and comfort come together. The ideal place to escape the hustle and bustle and recharge completely.
This villa is perfect for families, groups of friends (25+), but also perfect for a yoga weekend or a full retreat week. Surrounded by greenery and nature, situated in a quiet location and near several golf courses, you will experience pure relaxation here.
Enjoy the private swimming pool (heated in winter optionally for a fee), generous and attractive outdoor areas, a lovely sauna and breathtaking views that immediately give you a sense of tranquillity. Inside, you will find a spacious living room, 4 comfortable bedrooms and 4 bathrooms and plenty of space for everyone.
A car is highly recommended: within a short time you can be in Málaga, Marbella or Torremolinos. This way you combine the best of both worlds: ultimate tranquillity and all the highlights of the Costa del Sol at your fingertips.
La Perla Escondida is not a place where you just stay, it is also a place where you really unwind.
