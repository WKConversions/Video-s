# Casa Benito
## Casa Benito with private pool & the beach
### Fantastic flat in the centre of Benalmádena, ideally located and within walking distance of the beach. The flat can accommodate up to 4 people.
Enjoy a lovely stay in this cosy flat, just a few minutes' walk from Playa de Santa Ana beach. Located on the ground floor, the accommodation features a ground-level terrace overlooking the garden and seasonal swimming pool. Check-in is via clear instructions from the host, via a key box.
The flat is fully equipped with everything you need:
An atmospheric bedroom
Comfortable living room overlooking the terrace and pool to relax with flat-screen TV
Fully equipped kitchen with fridge and coffee machine
Spacious bathroom with lounger and toilet (+bidet)
Cooling air conditioning throughout the flat
Free wifi throughout the accommodation
Free private parking available at the apartment
#### The location is perfect:
A few minutes' walk to Playa de Santa Ana beach
And within walking distance of the cosy port with its delicious Spanish eateries
Only 18 km from Málaga airport
This central location is the ideal base for exploring Benalmádena and the Costa del Sol.
Whether you want to relax by the pool, stroll along the promenade or explore the area, here you will feel right at home and enjoy the wonderful Spanish life.
