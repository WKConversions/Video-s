# Málaga Hideaway: plan

**Brief.** 46 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, English, Karl's ElevenLabs voice-over (Christina, energetic). Audience: owners of a home
in Málaga who live elsewhere (the site speaks Dutch, English, German, Spanish). Message: one local partner takes every
moving part off your hands, and you stay in the loop. Motion norm 4% (median share of the frame in motion). Music and
effects over the whole film.

**Thesis.** One home, a hundred moving parts: they swarm around it while you are far away; Málaga Hideaway takes them
in hand, one by one, until all that is left is the home and your freedom to enjoy it.

**Thread object.** The home card: a real photo of a home Málaga Hideaway manages, with a status chip that changes
with the story (Booked, Guests arriving, Cleaned, Ready for every stay, Covered while you're away, Ready for you).

**Allowed patterns.** The home card and a few hospitality UI cards (calendar, check-in code, cleaning checklist,
repair ticket, messages, agreement, service switches, updates); one map (where the owner is vs. where the home is);
real footage only where the line is about the real world (the local partner, arrivals, cleaning, repairs, freedom);
colour fields that grow out of an object; words on screen only for the brand line and the call to action.

**Motion identity.** Warm and confident. Signature curve: soft ease-out arrivals (expo-out, no overshoot); one move
per hand-off; durations 12 / 18 / 24 f; entrances rise out of a light blur. Sounds: each made once, in the music's key.
