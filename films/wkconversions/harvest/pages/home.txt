How it works
From brief to final export.
A clear process, with your feedback built in at every step.
01
Brief
We start with what the video needs to do: who watches it, where it will live and what people should understand or do afterwards. You get a clear plan and a quote before anything is made.
02
Script & storyboard
We write the script and sketch the key frames, so you can see the story before a single frame is animated. Feedback here is cheap and fast.
03
Design & animation
We design the look and animate it in After Effects, then edit and finish in DaVinci Resolve: pacing, sound, subtitles and colour.
04
Delivery
You get final files in every format you need, 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16, with two revision rounds included along the way. The files are yours to keep.

Questions
Answered before you ask.
What does your motion design service cover?
Motion design is our speciality. We design and animate explainer and onboarding sequences, product and UI walkthroughs, website hero animations, brand and logo animation, kinetic typography and animated social content. Everything is produced in Adobe After Effects with 2D and 3D animation, and exported natively for where it will be used, in 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16.
Where can we use the videos?
Almost anywhere. One video can run in the hero of your homepage, explain a service on its own page, walk people through your product, or go out as social media content and social media ads. Because we cut every piece into the formats you need, one project can cover several of these at once.
Do you film on location?
No. Our edits are built from material you already own: screen recordings, your website and product pages, app screens, recorded demos, existing footage and brand assets. For digital products, SaaS and e-commerce this is usually the strongest approach, and it keeps production fast and costs predictable. If extra screen captures or clips are needed, we send you a creative brief for them.
How much does a video cost?
Every project is quoted on request, because the price depends on length, complexity and the number of formats and versions. Send us a message through the contact form, message Raphael on LinkedIn, or email or message Karl on Instagram, and you receive a clear quote within 24 hours. Quotes exclude VAT.

Use the contact form, message Raphael on LinkedIn, or email or message Karl on Instagram. We respond within 24 hours with concrete next steps and a quote for your project.
