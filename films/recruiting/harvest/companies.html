Why It Works

  • Publishing an assignment costs nothing. No retainer, no monthly fee, no minimum volume. You pay the reward first when the candidate starts the job.

  • You set the reward yourself, and it lands well below the 20 to 25% of annual salary that agencies invoice. Our fee is inside that reward, never added on top.

  • The technology finds. The recruiter judges. Every candidate you see has been interviewed and motivated by a specialist in your industry.

  • Your assignment goes out to recruiters who already work in your niche and your market, so the first candidates arrive in days instead of weeks.
