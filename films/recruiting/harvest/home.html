For Companies

20 daysaverage time to hire
From published role to a signed hire in 20 days on average, instead of the six to eight weeks an agency search takes. Candidates arrive within days; from there your own process sets the pace.
30-45%lower cost than an agency
You set the reward yourself, and it lands below the 20 to 25% of annual salary agencies invoice. Our fee sits inside that reward.
7/10match score before a candidate reaches you
Anyone scoring under the line is held back, unless the recruiter makes a deliberate case for them, and then reviewed by our team before you see them. The technology finds and ranks, the recruiter judges, and a specialist has interviewed everyone you do see.
Oneplace for the whole process
Ad writing, screening questions, kanban pipeline, interview booking and messages all sit in the same platform.
