# Automatic gearbox of the 2001 BMW 330Ci coupe (E46)

Topic folder: `gearbox`. Images: `/home/user/Video-s/films/bmw-e46/assets/ref/gearbox/`.
The car (RDW): 330Ci coupe, M54B30, 2979 cc, 170 kW / 300 Nm, first registered 9 April 2001, EU type approval, automatic (per owner).

## Main finding: which gearbox?

**ZF 5HP19, called "A5S 325Z" by BMW. It is NOT the GM 5L40-E.**

* BMW's own E46 training manual gives the production dates for the ZF A5S 325Z (5HP19): "330i/Ci/Cic: from 6/00". The application chart lists "A5S 325Z ... 325i/330i (E46) M54 6/00-" [S1 p.33, p.53].
* The BMW parts catalogue (ETK) for a **European** E46 330Ci coupe, left-hand drive, 2001 production lists only "Automatic transmission A5S325Z" (BMW 24 00 1 423 933, "A5S 325Z - TU"), valid up to 03/2005. It also lists A5S325Z oil pan, housing, wiring and pressure regulators, and the EGS control unit "GS8.60.4" [S6a, S6b].
* Wikipedia's 5HP19 list includes "2001-2003 BMW E46 330Ci M54B30" [S7].
* The GM 5L40-E (BMW names: A5S 360R / A5S 390R, "GM5") was built by GM Powertrain (Hydramatic) in Strasbourg, France. On the E46 it was used in the 323i/328i (1998-2000), the 325xi/330xi four-wheel-drive models, and the 325it (9/00-3/01). It was not used in the rear-wheel-drive 330i/330Ci of 2001 [S1 p.3-4, p.53; S2 p.4; S3; S4 p.52; S8].
* Later: in the USA the 325i/330i went over to the GM A5S390R from 3/2003 production [S10]. The European ETK lists the A5S325Z for the EU 330Ci up to 03/2005. This does not affect our 2001 car.

## Plain-Dutch summary (voice-over / captions)

Deze 330Ci heeft een automaat met vijf versnellingen van ZF uit Saarbrücken: de 5HP19, die BMW "A5S 325Z" noemt.
In plaats van een koppelingspedaal zit er een koppelomvormer: een met olie gevulde "ventilator-in-een-doos" die de motor soepel laat wegrijden zonder af te slaan. Boven ongeveer 25 km/h kan een klein koppelingsplaatje erin de motor en de bak vast aan elkaar zetten.
Daarachter zitten planetaire tandwielsets: een zonnewiel in het midden, planeetwielen eromheen en een ring buitenom. Olie-gestuurde koppelingen en remmen houden steeds een ander deel vast, en zo ontstaan vijf versnellingen vooruit en een achteruit.
Een computer (de EGS) kiest zelf wanneer hij schakelt. Met Steptronic duw je de pook naar links en schakel je zelf: naar voren is opschakelen (+), naar achteren terugschakelen (-).

## Modelling notes (estimates unless a source is given)

Coordinate system as in body.md: x = mm rearward from the front-axle centre, y = mm from the car centre line, z = mm above the ground.

**Overall shape (side view, front to back)**
1. Bell housing (torque converter housing): cast aluminium, bolted flat against the back of the engine block. Flange outline roughly a rounded square/oval with bolt bosses around the rim, about 440 mm wide and 420 mm tall (L). The input axis sits lower than the middle of the flange: flange bottom about 175 mm below the axis, top about 240-250 mm above (M from the BMW section; top cut off in the drawing). Behind the flange the bell is a short drum about 330-350 mm across around the converter, then it tapers like a funnel. Bell depth (flange to the start of the main case) about 145-170 mm.
2. Torque converter inside the bell: a fat doughnut/disc, shell about 275 mm diameter x about 110 mm thick, centred on the crank axis (W254 = 254 mm nominal; shell size measured on the BMW section drawing).
3. Main case: cast aluminium, a tapered cylinder with longitudinal ribs, about 230 mm diameter at the front narrowing to about 180 mm at the back, about 400 mm long. Top of the main case about 100 mm above the input axis.
4. Tail (extension) housing: a short cone, about 110-130 mm long, narrowing to about 110 mm diameter at the output bearing.
5. Output flange: three-arm flange, about 100-115 mm across, about 25 mm thick, then the rubber flex disc.
6. Oil pan: flat, ribbed metal tray under the main case, about 430 mm long x about 290 mm wide x about 60-90 mm deep, starting about 125 mm behind the bell flange. Bottom of the pan about 215 mm below the input axis. Fill plug on the left side of the pan near the rear; drain plug in the bottom.
7. Left side details: selector shaft lever with the cable bracket, the black electrical connector, and two silver oil-cooler pipes running forward.

**Overall size**: about 700 mm long from the bell flange to the output flange face (about 730 mm to the end of the output stub), about 440 mm wide at the bell flange, about 230-250 mm wide at the main case, and about 430-460 mm tall overall from the pan bottom to the top of the bell.
*How the length was found (M):* the BMW section drawing (ST034 p.35) was scaled with the 254 mm converter (0.58 mm per pixel). It gives 690-700 mm. Cross-check: the automatic drive shaft is 147 mm shorter than the manual one (1403 vs 1550 mm), and the engine flange (x ~375) + 700 + 1403 mm puts the final-drive flange about 250 mm ahead of the rear axle (x ~2475 of 2725), which is believable. No official ZF/BMW length figure was found.

**Position in the car (L-M)**
* Bell flange at x about +350 to +400 (from the engine notes: rear block flange 0.35-0.40 m behind the front axle). Output flange at about x +1050 to +1100. The gearbox runs back under the centre tunnel, between the footwells and the front of the front seats.
* On the car centre line (y = 0) [S4]. Input axis = crank axis, about z 360-380 (engine notes). Pan bottom then about z 150-165 (engine oil pan bottom about z 140). Main case top about z 470; top of the bell about z 600.
* Orientation: the axis is lengthwise along the car, nearly level (perhaps 1-2 degrees nose-up, i.e. sloping down to the rear; not verified). The engine leans about 30 degrees to the right, but the gearbox sits upright with its pan flat at the bottom; only the bell flange bolt pattern follows the tilted block (M, from the BMW drawings and normal BMW practice).
* Gearbox crossmember under the tail housing at about x +950 to +1000, with two rubber mounts.
* Selector lever on the centre console, roughly above the rear half of the gearbox (about x +1000 to +1150), linked to the left side of the gearbox.
* Drive shaft: from the output flange (x ~1075) back to the final drive flange (x ~2475): 1403 mm. Flex disc right at the gearbox (black rubber ring, 6 bolts, about 130 mm diameter). Centre bearing about 45 % of the way back (x ~1700), bolted to the floor with a bracket. Universal joint just behind the centre bearing. Rear tube to a CV joint (about 105 mm diameter, black rubber boot) bolted to the diff flange. Tubes about 60-75 mm diameter, front tube stepped (L).

**Colours / materials**
* Bell housing and main case: dull cast aluminium, light grey #A3A39C with darker grime #6E6E66 in the ribs.
* Oil pan: ribbed metal, mid-dark grey #55575A (L: steel or aluminium not confirmed).
* Torque converter (if shown cut away): bright steel #B8BCC0 with a gold/brown friction-lining ring for the lock-up clutch.
* Planetary gears (cut-away beat): steel #C8CCD0; clutch packs alternating dark friction plates #4A3B2E and steel plates.
* Output flange: dark steel #3A3A3A. Flex disc: black rubber #151515 with silver bolts. Drive shaft tubes: dark grey/black #2B2B2B. CV boot: black. Centre bearing: black rubber in a silver bracket.

**Recognisable features (to make it read as "the automatic")**: the big round bell with the converter doughnut inside, the long ribbed case tapering to the back, the flat ribbed oil pan underneath, and the three-arm output flange with the black rubber flex disc. Inside the car: the P-R-N-D lever with the M/S gate to its left.

**Film ideas from the facts**: show the converter as two facing fans in oil (impeller drives turbine); show a planetary set with sun/planets/ring and colour-flash which part is held per gear; show the lock-up clutch closing above 25 km/h; show the Steptronic lever going left, then forward = +.
