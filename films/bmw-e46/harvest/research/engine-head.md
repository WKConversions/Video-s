# Engine and cylinder head: BMW M54B30 in the 2001 E46 330Ci coupe

Topic folder: `engine-head`. Images: `/home/user/Video-s/films/bmw-e46/assets/ref/engine-head/`.
The car (RDW): 330Ci coupe, 2979 cc, 6 cylinders, 170 kW, first registered 9 Apr 2001, automatic (per owner).
The engine is the same M54B30 with manual or automatic gearbox. The engine facts below do not change with the gearbox.

## Plain-Dutch summary (for the voice-over / captions)

De 330Ci heeft een 3,0-liter zescilinder-lijnmotor, de BMW M54B30: 231 pk (170 kW) en 300 Nm.
De cilinderkop is het aluminium "dak" op het motorblok. Erin zitten twee nokkenassen en 24 kleppen.
De kleppen laten links verse lucht met benzine binnen en rechts de verbrande gassen naar buiten.
In het midden van elke verbrandingskamer zit een bougie.
De motor ligt in de lengterichting voorin, iets naar rechts gekanteld, onder een zwarte kunststof kap met het BMW-opschrift.

## What the cylinder head does (plain words)

EN: The head closes off the top of the six cylinders and forms the roof of each combustion chamber.
It holds the valves, which open and close like doors.
Two camshafts push the valves open at the right moment: on the left side to let air and fuel in, on the right side to let the burnt gas out.
A spark plug in the centre of each chamber lights the mixture.
Coolant channels in the head keep it cool.

NL: De cilinderkop sluit de zes cilinders van boven af en vormt het dak van elke verbrandingskamer.
Twee nokkenassen duwen de kleppen open als deurtjes: links stroomt lucht met benzine naar binnen, rechts gaan de verbrande gassen eruit.
In het midden zit de bougie die het mengsel ontsteekt.

## Modelling notes (stylised model)

These dimensions are ESTIMATES, built from bore spacing 91 mm, bore 84 mm and stroke 89.6 mm, plus photo proportions. BMW does not publish package dimensions in the sources found.

- Engine axes: X = forward, Y = left, Z = up. The crank axis runs along the car centre line.
- **Block**: box about 620 L x 300 W x 420 H mm (crank axis to deck about 215 mm; skirt about 70 mm below crank). Raw cast aluminium (#A8A8A3). Cylinders 1-6 centres 91 mm apart (cyl 1 to cyl 6 = 455 mm), cyl 1 nearest the front.
- **Oil pan**: aluminium, about 560 L x 260 W x 150 H mm, deeper at the front.
- **Cylinder head**: about 640 L x 270 W x 145 H mm, cast aluminium. Front face carries the VANOS housing: an aluminium box about 220 W x 120 H x 60 deep, high on the front of the head over both cam ends.
- **Valve cover**: black plastic (#151515), about 650 L x 240 W x 70 H, rounded edges.
- **Coil cover**: black plastic on the right half, about 560 x 120 x 30 mm, with 8-10 silver horizontal ribs (#B5B5B5) and raised "BMW" letters near the front third. A second ribbed cover (about 520 x 90 mm) sits beside it on the left over the injectors.
- **Oil filler cap**: black cylinder Ø60 x 30 mm on the valve cover near the front, right side.
- **Spark plugs/coils**: 6 vertical stubs in a line on the head centre line, hidden under the coil cover.
- **Timing case and front pulleys**: aluminium cover on the block front; crank pulley/damper Ø about 180 mm; water pump with viscous fan (black, Ø about 420 mm, 7-9 blades) high at the front centre; alternator front left low.
- **Intake manifold (left side)**: black plastic. A rectangular plenum runs along the left side at about head height, about 600 L x 140 W x 140 H. Six runners (about 50 mm Ø) leave the plenum, arch up and over, and curve back down into the left face of the head. It looks like six parallel "candy-cane" loops seen from the front. Throttle body and intake boot (black rubber, about 80 mm Ø) at the front-left end, going forward to the airbox in the front-left corner of the bay. Red oil-dipstick handle on the left front.
- **Exhaust (right side)**: two cast manifolds, three short pipes each, merging into two catalytic converter cans (Ø about 110 x 250 mm). The cans point down and back along the right side of the block. Dark grey/rust (#4A4038), silver heat shields.
- **Tilt**: rotate the whole engine about the crank axis by about 30° (low confidence), with the top leaning to the RIGHT (exhaust side down, intake side up).
- **Position in the car**: crank axis on the car centre line, about 360-380 mm above the ground (oil-pan bottom about 140 mm above the ground). Front wheel centre is about 316 mm high (tyre about 632 mm). Lengthwise (BMW press drivetrain top view, measured): crank pulley about 0.35-0.45 m AHEAD of the front-axle centre line, cylinder 3/4 roughly over the axle, rear block flange/bellhousing about 0.35-0.40 m BEHIND the axle. Gearbox continues back into the tunnel. Front overhang is 774 mm, so the fan and radiator fill the space between the engine and the bumper.
- **Highlight inside the car**: colour the head (aluminium band between block and valve cover) plus the valve cover. When it is pulled out, lift it straight up, rotated back to upright (undo the tilt). Show cut-away cams (2 long rods with 12 lobes each), 24 valves in two inclined rows (about 20° from vertical each side, V-shape) and the central plug.
- **Recognisable features**: the "BMW" ribbed coil cover, the two parallel ribbed covers, the six curved intake runners on the left, the silver VANOS box at the front of the head, and the round black oil cap at the front right.

## Open questions

- Exact M54 tilt angle in the E46: about 30° to the right is the forum consensus and BMW's general six-cylinder heritage. No primary BMW figure was found.
- Engine position vs front axle was measured from one press photo with perspective. The likely error is about ±80 mm.
- Engine weight: 121 kg (de.wikipedia, unsourced) vs 126 kg (unixnerd). There is no BMW figure.
- Cam durations for the M54B30 are unverified.
- Resonance-flap switch point: 3750 rpm (BMW ST055) vs about 4100 rpm (australiancar.reviews).
- Valve angles (20°15'/19°15') are documented for the M50 head; it is assumed, not verified, that they carry over to the M54.
- We do not know whether the brother's car still has the original black engine covers (it has aftermarket wheels). Check under the bonnet.
