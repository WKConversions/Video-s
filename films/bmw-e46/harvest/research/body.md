# Topic: the unique E46 Coupe body style (folder: body)

Car: BMW 330Ci E46 Coupe (E46/2), automatic, first registered 9 April 2001 (pre-facelift coupe), black, RDW variant BN51.
Researched 2026-10-05. Every number below has its source next to it. Where sources disagree, both are given.

## Short version (Dutch, for on-screen / voice-over)

De E46 Coupe lijkt op de sedan, maar is een compleet eigen carrosserie: BMW zegt dat er geen enkel plaatwerkdeel gedeeld wordt, en aan de buitenkant alleen de deurgrepen, de zijknipperlichten en de BMW-logo's. Hij is 46 mm lager, 17 mm langer en 18 mm breder dan de sedan, met een 2 graden schuinere voorruit, een korter dak en lange deuren met raamloze ruiten die automatisch een stukje zakken als je de deur opent. Ontworpen door Erik Goplen (DesignworksUSA), gepresenteerd op de Autosalon van Geneve in maart 1999, gebouwd in Regensburg.

### Modelling measurements (from the official BMW ST034 E46/2 dimension drawing, measured by scaling)
Coordinate system: x = mm rearward from front-axle centre, z = mm above ground, y = mm from centreline. Accuracy about +/-20 mm.

* Front bumper tip x = -774 (label; drawing measures ~-755). Rear bumper x = +3714.
* Wheel/tyre: 225/45 R17 -> outer diameter 634 mm (calc.), width 225 mm, rim 432 mm (17"). Axle height ~310-317.
* Arch openings: front top z ~690-700, rear top z ~665-675; opening reaches ~+/-380-390 mm fore/aft of axle at sill height. Thin flare line ~50 mm above arch.
* Sill bottom z ~160-175; front bumper bottom z ~180-190; rear bumper bottom ~225-235; rear bumper top ~610.
* Headlight: x -690..-390, z ~585-710; front view lamp centres y +/-425 and +/-597, z ~632.
* Kidney pair: total ~545 mm wide, each ~250 wide, z ~550-700, roundel on bonnet z ~735.
* Bonnet: front edge z ~720-740, rising to cowl at x ~+450 / z ~945; bonnet length ~1150-1200 along centreline.
* Windscreen: base x ~+450 z ~945; top x ~+1210 z ~1330 -> side-view rake ~27 deg from horizontal (~63 deg from vertical).
* Roof: max z 1369 around x +1600..+1900; roof length (header to rear-window top) ~1320.
* Rear window: top x ~+2530 z ~1325, bottom x ~+3150..+3200 (sides; centre ~+3300) z ~1045 -> ~24-25 deg.
* Boot lid deck: x ~+3200..+3630, z ~1060 -> ~1000 (slight down-slope), lip at the rear edge.
* Belt line (window sill): z ~900-910 at A-pillar rising to ~955 at C-pillar. Shoulder crease: z ~700 at headlight, ~800 at front of door, ~860 above tail light.
* Mirror: x ~+830..+975, z ~910..1055, out to y +/-974.
* Door (the drawing shows the door transparent, so these come from the free side-profile photo, scaled by the 2725 mm wheelbase, about +/-5%): front shut line x ~+680 (just behind the front arch), rear shut line almost vertical at x ~+1930 (sill) to ~+2030 (belt). Door length ~1.2-1.25 m. Sedan front door in the BMW drawing: ~1.0 m (x ~+620..+1610).
* B-pillar: slim black, flush strip at x ~+2000-2030 between the frameless door glass and the rear quarter glass; the whole side-window shape has a bright (chrome) surround, so it reads as one long glass area.
* Rear quarter window: x ~+2030..+2750 at belt height, Hofmeister kink at the lower rear corner, x ~+2700-2750, z ~920-1090 (drawing and photo agree).
* Side repeater: x ~+400..+500, z ~600. Door handle: x ~+1700..+1780.
* Tail light (side): x ~+3410..+3620, z ~700..960; rear view: cluster from y +/-345 to +/-740, z ~690..880, split at the boot-lid gap at y ~+/-515.
* Exhaust: twin round tips on the car's left, y ~-480..-560, z ~290.
* Front view: body width 1757 at z ~450-520; greenhouse ~1500 wide at belt -> ~1000 at roof (strong tumblehome). Fog lamps y +/-540, z ~360, dia ~80.
* Plan view: front half-width ~700 at 100 mm behind tip, ~830 at 300 mm, full by ~500 mm; rear full width to ~4000 from tip, ~800 at 4300, ~600 at 4450.

## Open questions
* Exact drop distance of the frameless door glass (not published by BMW in the sources found).
* Paint code of the brother's car (black: Schwarz II 668 solid vs Saphirschwarz/Black Sapphire 475 metallic) - check the sticker in the B-pillar/door jamb or under the bonnet.
* Whether the brother's car's corona rings are a retrofit (factory angel eyes on the coupe only from 03/2003).
* Size of the aftermarket 10-spoke wheels (stock is 8Jx17 with 225/45 R17).
* Official ground clearance figure.
* Cd 0.30 (BMW AG) vs 0.32 (BMW NA) - use "about 0.30" on screen or avoid the number.
* Cross-topic note for the gearbox/diff researchers: the BMW NA 2001 spec sheet lists the ZF 5HP19 for the 330Ci coupe automatic (not the GM 5L40-E), and the German BMW data sheet gives "Hinterachsuebersetzung 2,93 (3,38)" for the 330Ci (value in brackets = automatic per the sheet's footnote); BMW NA lists 2.93:1 for both. Needs checking by those topics.
