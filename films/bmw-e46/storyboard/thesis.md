# Thesis

Your brother's own black 330Ci turns in a bright studio; its body turns to glass, and the parts that make it a 330Ci
light up blue where they sit inside the car, step out to show how they work, and go back: the coupé body, the
cylinder head, the Double VANOS, the automatic gearbox and the rear differential, in the order the power flows.

**Allowed patterns:** the car rotating and floating free of the floor (asked for: "roteert op alle assen"); the paint
dissolving into x-ray glass along a sweep line with a thin blue seam, and coming back the same way; the car's own
lines drawn on the glass like a technical drawing; parts in BMW blue (#1C69D4) while pointed at; a callout line from
the part to its label; the sedan's roofline as a ghost line (body beat, from BMW's sedan drawing); a light pulse
travelling along the propshaft (power flow, gearbox → differential); the plate 25-KDB-4 and the dealer holder as on
the real car; one title, one line and one spec per part, all numbers from harvest/facts.md.
