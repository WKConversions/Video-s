# Missing (what a product film would want and the site doesn't have)

- **Real product screenshots or a screen recording of gtm.clearscaler.com.** Every product view on the site is a coded HTML mockup of a "Demo workspace" (Wrenmoor Industrial, fictional companies); the app itself sits behind https://gtm.clearscaler.com/sign-in. Work-around: rebuild the site's own mockups in the film (same layout, tokens, copy and data, assets/site/demo-workspace.json and mockups/*.png as references) and keep the "Demo workspace" pill in the URL bar, as the site does. Never present them as a client's live account. If Karl can get real (anonymised) app captures, they replace nothing until approved.
- **A brand guide or logo files.** None published. The logo exists only as inline SVG in the nav (mark + wordmark, currentColor) and favicon.svg. Work-around: assets/logo/clearscaler-logo*.svg rebuilt 1:1 from that markup; brand values measured from the live site and its CSS tokens (brand.json).
- **Customer names behind the numbers.** Only Top Jobs Abroad is named with figures (and a quote). The 8% reply rate (the only GTM Engine/outbound result), the 3x→5x / 6x / $15k→$100k / ~90% / +20% (UAE travel logistics) and 57→99 (Dubai brand) are anonymised. Work-around: show them exactly as the site labels them ("A B2B services company · first month of sending", "Real · anonymised") with the measure/window/source line small beneath; no invented logos or names next to them.
- **Customer logos with outcomes.** The logo wall's nine clients (Oftet, Top Jobs Abroad, KB Consultancy, YouNomad, bFound, Talentify, Marzelle Sweets, WK Conversions, Coastal Designs) agreed to be named, but the site ties no result to eight of them. Work-around: show them as the site does, a monochrome grey row under "CLIENTS WHO AGREED TO BE NAMED", never paired with a number. Several are tiny raster files (40 to 72 px high source; KB Consultancy and Talentify are knockout tiles): keep them small on screen (about 40 px tall at 1080p), as the mono renders in assets/clients/mono/ show.
- **More outbound proof.** The GTM Engine, which the film is about, has one real result (8% reply rate over 300 contacts in one month, plus 55% open rate on /results). No meetings-booked count, pipeline value or revenue from outbound, and the site says outright "We don't promise a meeting count." Work-around: the film's GTM numbers are the demo workspace's (labelled demo) plus the single 8% claim; any "meetings booked" number on screen must be demo data (e.g. the weekly summary's "3 meetings booked", demo).
- **Integrations and tools.** The site names no CRM, email-sending, enrichment or calendar vendors (only Meta, Google and LinkedIn for ads, Lighthouse for scores, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, "your connected mailbox", "your calendar"). Work-around: no third-party logos; show generic "your mailbox" / "your calendar" UI as the mockups do.
- **Pricing.** None ("One build fee, one retainer, tools included"; "Numbers on the call"). Work-around: if the film touches cost, use the structure line, not a figure.
- **Founder imagery.** Only two small head-and-shoulders photos: inline 128 px avatars (dark background, used everywhere on the site) and 400 px PNGs referenced by the site's data (/images/magnus.jpg on grey, /images/kian.jpg cut out on transparent; inconsistent backgrounds). No video, no team or office photos. Work-around: founders appear as the site shows them, small round avatars (≤ 96 px at 1080p from the 400 px files, circle-cropped on a night/surface-1 disc) with name and "Co-founder · Oslo to Málaga" lines; not full-frame portraits.
- **Photography and illustration in general.** The site has none beyond the avatars: its imagery is UI mockups, the loop diagram, hairline grids and type. Work-around: none needed; the film stays in that language (no stock footage).
- **A logo animation or motion guide.** None; but the site's own motion vocabulary is rich (comet on the loop, word-by-word typing, strike/insert, approval stamp, toast, score count-up, loop-field twinkle; assets/site/diagrams.md). Work-around: build the logo reveal from the hero loop: the hairline loop is the same curve as the logo mark (offset by 103.2, 326), so the comet lap can resolve into the stroked mark.
- **Sound, voice, music.** Not needed: the film is silent.
- **A product name for the app.** The site calls it "The GTM app" (nav) and "our own app" / "your own workspace"; the service is "GTM Engine". Work-around: use those exact names; don't coin a brand name for the app.
- **The → glyph in the site fonts.** The woff2 subsets (Outfit, Manrope, Geist Mono) don't include U+2192, so "3x → 5x" falls back to a system font on the site. Work-around: draw arrows as the site's SVG arrow (shaft "M2.5 8h10", head "M9 4.5 12.5 8 9 11.5", stroke 1.75, round caps) or in orange as on /results.
- **Outfit weights.** The Outfit subset only covers weights 500 to 600 (Manrope 200 to 800, Geist Mono 400 to 600). Work-around: headlines at 600 (as the site), H2 at 560; nothing bolder.
