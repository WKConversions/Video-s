How we did it
That did not come from clever copy. It came from the work before the copy: dedicated sending domains, warmed mailboxes, and a verified list built to a tight buyer profile rather than bought in bulk.
Three hundred hand-picked contacts on a four-step sequence is not a volume play, it is phase one, and it is deliberately expensive. What it buys is a readable result: which buyers replied, which angle landed, which offer produced meetings rather than polite answers.
The winners then get templatised, the research and drafting get automated, and volume goes up on the version that earned it.

What founders ask before they start.
Where do the leads and meetings show up?
Booked meetings land straight in your calendar with context on who they are and why they replied. Every conversation lives in one pipeline you can see whenever you want.
We tried outbound before. Why would this work?
Most attempts fade because nobody owns them week to week. We run the engine and tune it weekly on replies and booked calls, so it compounds instead of fading.
Do we have to write anything or approve every send?
You never have to write anything. Our system drafts, a person on our side edits every message, and in the early months you approve every send before it goes out. That is deliberate: every approval, edit and rejection trains the engine on your market, and as the pattern gets clear your approvals narrow to the exceptions.
Will this hurt our domain or brand?
Outreach runs on separate sending domains we register and warm for you. Your main domain is never touched. Copy is written to sound like a person from your team, not a blast, and volume ramps gradually.
