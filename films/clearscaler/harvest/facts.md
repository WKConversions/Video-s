## Process steps (verbatim)
Home [H], "Found. Written. Booked." (rail: 01 Found · 02 Written · 03 Booked):
1. "Find and score." "Every account researched, every contact scored against your rubric, with the reason written down." Proof line: "We check whether our own scores predict replies."
2. "Write and approve." "Written per person, edited by us, approved by you in the early months."
3. "Reply and book." "Every reply classified, an answer drafted, sent from your mailbox in the same thread."

GTM Engine [E], "INSIDE THE ENGINE" (01 Find · 02 Write · 03 Protect · 04 Reply · 05 Learn · 06 Report):
1. "Find and score." (same body as home) + "We check whether our own scores predict replies."
2. "Write and approve." "One argument per sequence, written per person, checked before it can send."
3. "Your name stays safe." "Separate sending domains, warmed for four weeks before anything sends."
4. "Reply and book." "Every reply classified, an answer drafted, sent from your mailbox in the same thread." + "Replies go out from your real mailbox, in the same thread."
5. "Learn." "Offers, subject lines and copy tested on replies and meetings, never on opens." + "Winners are called on replies, with the odds shown."
6. "Report." "A Monday summary of what went out, who replied and what got booked."

Logistics [L] variant, "Find. Reach. Book. Run for you, every week.": Find "We map the shippers on your lanes worth reaching and score every contact before a word is written." · Reach "Written per person, edited by us, approved by you in the early months." · Book "Meetings land in your calendar with the research and the thread attached." · "Shipper calls you take, not a number we promise." (Other audience pages swap "shippers on your lanes" for "hiring companies" [RA], "best-fit clients" [MA], "in-market accounts" [SA], "buyers" [B2B], and "Shipper calls" for "BD calls" / "New-business calls" / "Demos" / "Meetings".)

Who writes (FAQ, [H] [E] and all audience pages): "You never have to write anything. Our system drafts, a person on our side edits every message, and in the early months you approve every send before it goes out. That is deliberate: every approval, edit and rejection trains the engine on your market, and as the pattern gets clear your approvals narrow to the exceptions."
Domain safety (FAQ): "Outreach runs on separate sending domains we register and warm for you. Your main domain is never touched. Copy is written to sound like a person from your team, not a blast, and volume ramps gradually."
Ownership (FAQ [E]): "You do. Every lead, every sending domain, every workflow and all the campaign history. If you leave, it leaves with you."
