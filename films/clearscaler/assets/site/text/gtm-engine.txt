Before you ask
What does it cost?
The build fee is scoped on how many sending domains and mailboxes you need, the size of the list and how many channels run. The retainer is one number per month and holds while that setup holds. Nothing is charged per send and there are no tiers. You get both numbers, in writing, before anything is built.
Who writes the emails, and do I approve every send?
You never have to write anything. Our system drafts, a person on our side edits every message, and in the early months you approve every send before it goes out. That is deliberate: every approval, edit and rejection trains the engine on your market, and as the pattern gets clear your approvals narrow to the exceptions.
How long before the first meeting is booked?
Weeks one to four we build and warm, and nothing sends. The first campaigns go out around week four. First replies usually arrive in the first weeks of sending. Anyone promising booked meetings in week one is either sending from a cold domain or sending on somebody else's. Meetings you show up to, not a number we promise.
Will this hurt our domain or brand?
Outreach runs on separate sending domains we register and warm for you. Your main domain is never touched. Copy is written to sound like a person from your team, not a blast, and volume ramps gradually.
Who owns the data and the domains?
You do. Every lead, every sending domain, every workflow and all the campaign history. If you leave, it leaves with you.
Can you run just one channel?
Yes, and we will run it properly. Connected, the three compound: outbound replies tell you the real objection in the buyer's own words within weeks, that objection becomes the ad angle, paid tests it at volume in days, and what converts on the page tells both channels which promise is landing.
