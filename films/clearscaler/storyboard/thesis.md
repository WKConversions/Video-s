# Thesis

Thesis: ClearScaler's GTM Engine works your whole market for you, shown on one dark map of that market: every account is
researched and scored, the ones that fit are written to one person at a time with your approval, and the replies come back
as meetings in your calendar.

Allowed patterns:
- The market map: an isometric field of dark blocks, each block an account, as an illustration of the viewer's market
  (Karl's reference film's way of visualizing: the domain drawn as a live model). The blocks are unnamed except the
  demo workspace's companies.
- The site's demo workspace and its fictional companies and people (Fernhollow Freight / Sofia Berglund, Quillmoor Group /
  Marta Vogel, Brightwick Systems / Daniel Halvorsen, Larkspan Analytics / Leo Okafor, Pellbrook Supply / Tom Keller), their
  statuses, scores, email and replies, verbatim, with the chip labelled "Demo workspace" as every product view on the site is.
- A status chip (the GTM app's lead row) that flips through the app's own statuses.
- Counters only for the demo's 1,284 leads and 812 in outreach, the demo score 87 / 135, and the real 8% (labelled
  "Real · anonymised" with its measure and window, as the site labels it).
- Orange (#FC7F48) only for what ClearScaler does (the scan, the scores that pass, the emails going out, the CTA); green
  (#4BC680) only for replies and meetings, as in the app.
- One cursor, for the one click that belongs to the viewer: Approve & send.
- The ∞ mark as the 8 of 8% (the mark turned upright), which turns back into the logo.
- The founders' own photos at the end, as the site shows them next to "30 minutes with Magnus or Kian".
- The client's dark style (night #07080C ground), named in the brief; it overrides Karl's standing "bright by default".
