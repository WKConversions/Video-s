# Brief: ClearScaler, GTM platform film

Karl's prompt (5 Oct 2026): "I want a video for the business clearscaler.com in their dark style for their gtm platform.
No sound effects, no music, no voiceover. It has to be a video really visualizing what the platform is and does for you.
Be forward, strive to making the video, it takes too long normally." (He also attached a reference video for colour and
way of visualizing; it was posted in another chat and is not available in this session. The look comes from
ClearScaler's own dark site instead; see notes.)

PROJECT: ClearScaler — silent product film for the GTM Engine / GTM app (gtm.clearscaler.com).
CLIENT-BRAND: ClearScaler (clearscaler.com), dark site: night #07080C ground, warm white type, orange #FC7F48 accent,
  Outfit display, Manrope body, Geist Mono labels, the ∞ loop mark (brand.json, measured).
DELIVERABLE: MP4 + Remotion project (Karl can open it in Remotion Studio). Storyboard page alongside. Karl asked to be
  forward: storyboard and film are delivered together, no approval stop in between.
PLATFORM: website (hero / product section, muted autoplay), LinkedIn post. Plays muted by design.
ASPECT RATIO / RESOLUTION / FRAME RATE: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps (the website row in planning/intake.md; subject kept in the
  middle 60% for a later 9:16 cut).
TARGET DURATION: about 40 s. Silent, so on-screen words carry the narrative: every line must be read, not heard (reading
  time ≈ 3.5 words/s for short lines + 0.4 s to find the line).
AUDIENCE: founders and sales/marketing leads of B2B companies (recruitment, agencies, logistics, B2B SaaS) whose sales
  depend on the founder; busy, sceptical, 2 seconds to decide, watching muted.
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: make the viewer understand what the GTM platform is and what it does for them: it finds the right
  buyers, writes to each one personally, handles the replies and books the meetings into their calendar, run by
  ClearScaler, approved by them.
DESIRED VIEWER ACTION: book a call ("Book a call", 30 minutes with Magnus or Kian).
SCRIPT: no voice-over. The on-screen lines are written from the site's own copy (harvest/facts.md); see plan.md.
VOICE-OVER / MUSIC / SFX: none (Karl). The film must read entirely through picture and on-screen words.
BRAND ASSETS: harvested (assets/assets.md, brand.json): logo SVG, fonts, founders' photos, the loop diagram path.
PRODUCT ASSETS: the site's own product mockups of the GTM app (coded, demo workspace "Wrenmoor Industrial" with
  fictional companies, as the site itself labels them) — rebuilt in the film at readable size.
REFERENCES: Karl's reference set (references/technique-catalogue.md) and benchmark (references/benchmarks.md) for motion
  level; the reference video he attached elsewhere could not be seen here.
MUST INCLUDE: the platform doing the work, visibly: finding and scoring, writing and approving, replying and booking;
  the dark style; real numbers only.
MUST AVOID: a presentation (pages held while text explains); invented figures, clients or features; a generic SaaS
  dashboard; camera pans/zooms on transitions (K.B lesson); bounce; logo soup.
TONE: confident, precise, premium, a little dry (the site's voice: "We don't promise a meeting count."). Timing row:
  "confident and clear" → middle durations, ease-outs, no overshoot, one slow camera breath (motion/timing.md).
DEADLINE: this session (storyboard + finished film).
TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS: Remotion 4.0.532 in the sandbox, headless Chromium, 4 CPUs; chunked render for the final.
OTHER NOTES: Karl's standing direction is "bright by default, no static standard dark background"; this brief asks for
  the client's dark style by name, so the canvas is dark, and it is never static: light, depth and the orange field
  change with the story (thesis.md).

## Format decision
16:9 at 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 40 s: a product film for the site and LinkedIn (website hero/explainer row, 30–60 s).
Silent, so the length is set by reading time, not a voice.
