# Adrian Tineo — storyboard (39.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

Brief (Karl, 15 Oct): a film for Adrian Tineo's site, "completely in the style of the website" — the look and motion are
the site's (palette, Manrope two-tone headlines, eyebrows, white cards with pale wells, floating tags, chrome sculptures,
the paper shell over the dark ambient field, the one curve .22,1,.36,1), the visuals are new where the lines need it.
Voice-over at +0.50 s; word frames from `film/src/words.json`. Concepts A–C (this folder) were drafted in parallel; this
plan takes B's thread (one white card that keeps becoming the next thing), A's assembling sculpture for "built around
your business", and C's two-tone statement type.

**Thesis.** Adrian turns AI from scattered and complicated into one calm, working structure around your business.
**Thread object.** One white site card: Your business → Adrian → the right opportunity → strategy → service 01, joined by
siblings that bud out of it (solution, engineering/execution, the other services). Nothing appears from nothing.
**Frame.** The whole film lives inside the site's rounded paper shell over the ambient field, with the site's hero-art
top line (beat eyebrow, left; "0n — AT", right) and bottom line (YOUR AMBITION —rule→ APPLIED AI) as a quiet progress rule.
**Motion identity.** Calm and exact. One curve (EASE), reveals = fade + rise over 26 f with 3-f sibling steps, card
morphs on MOVE over 22–24 f, exits accelerate (DEPART, 12–14 f), floats 7 s / 9 s. No overshoot, no glow, median motion ≈ 4%.
