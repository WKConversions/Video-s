# Concept C: "Good questions. Clear answers." (editorial-typographic)

The film is one page of the site, set in its own type. It opens on a question and ends on a full stop. The word
**AI** fills the page in every size the site's type system has, then gathers into one question in the site's
two-tone headline: **"AI / for your business?"** The site's own FAQ questions pile up under it and the page loses
its order. Then the **point of the question mark** opens into Adrian's black-and-white portrait (the answer to the
question is a person), and closes again into the **full stop of his name**, "Adrian Tineo•", which docks as the
site's masthead. From there the hairline rules and the numbers do the work: the site's three approach rows are
uncovered, built and connected; a giant numeral rolls 01 to 04 through the four services while the site's
sculptures act out each verb; the same slot rolls into **Hype.** and **Guesswork.** and strikes them out; the
question comes back with its hook gone ("AI / for your business."); and the black plate of the site's hero
sculpture floods into the dark contact panel, where the film lands on the site's own answer: **"AI expertise. /
Business impact."** and **Let's talk ↗**.

Film: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 1170 frames (39.0 s), voice-over at +0.50 s. All times are film seconds and all frames are
film frames, frame = round(start × 30), read from `film/src/words.json`. All positions are px on the 1920×1080 frame:
text as left x and baseline y, boxes as x0–x1 / y0–y1.

## 2. Thesis, thread object, motion identity

### Thesis

**Adrian Tineo turns AI's open questions into clear answers for your business. The film shows it in the site's own
editorial type: the question mark of "your business?" opens into Adrian, becomes the full stop of his name, and
returns as the full stop that ends the question; the site's hairlines and numbers put the page in order around him.**

(The site's own FAQ heading says it: "Good questions. Clear answers.")

**Allowed patterns** (named here so the tells check can tell choices from reflexes):
- The site's type system as the main picture: two-tone headlines (ink #151514 + grey #777772; on the dark panel
  #F5F5F2 + #AAA9A1), weight 650 with tight negative tracking, tiny uppercase eyebrows with the sage status dot, the
  wordmark with its dot, numbered rows on hairlines, the site's ↗ ✳ symbols.
- **The giant slot**: one fixed left-hand slot of 280 px type whose content rolls (01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → Hype. →
  Guesswork. → AI). Its numerals are the site's service numbers 01–04, at film scale.
- The word "AI" set in five of the site's components (eyebrow, tag, dark tag, dark button, grey headline) in P1 only,
  to show "everywhere".
- The site's FAQ questions, shortened, as the "complicated" rows (P3).
- The site's three approach steps (01–03), rolled into the script's three disciplines (P6–P10), with a connector line
  and three points.
- The site's five sculptures (strategy cubes, build slabs, performance bars, linked rings, hero intelligence),
  rebuilt from the site's SVG files in separate parts so each acts out its verb. The bars carry no values.
- Adrian's portrait in black and white (grayscale(1), multiply, as on the site) with the site's frosted caption.
- One sage selection highlight (#BDD0C9, the site's `::selection` colour) on "opportunity." (P6). It is the only use.
- Two strike-throughs, on the script words "Hype." and "Guesswork." (P16–P17).
- The site's hero art panel with its four corner labels (P18–P20) and the site's dark contact panel with its faint arc,
  ↗ symbol, eyebrow and light button (P20–end).
- **Not used:** numbers or results, client names or logos (Goldman Sachs, Telepass, NVIDIA, A.Team, Málaga AI), quotes,
  testimonials, the test URL.

### Motion identity

**Personality: precise premium, the site's own.** Calm, exact, no overshoot, no bounce, no glow.

| Constant | Value |
|---|---|
| Signature curve | `EASE` = cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), the site's only curve: every arrival, reveal, roll, draw and unfold (about 85% of moves) |
| Held objects moving between states | `MOVE` = cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1): the AI re-setting, the portrait closing, the wordmark docking, rules converging, the card folding |
| Exits | `DEPART` = cubic-bezier(.55,0,.9,.4), at 65–75% of the matching entrance |
| Floats and the camera breath | `INOUT` (the site's `ease-in-out`): sculptures 9 s (−9 px, 1.5°), tags 7 s (−6 px) |
| Duration palette (frames) | **quick 6–10** (exits, strikes, colour changes, the point landing); **standard 12–16** (word rises, rolls, rule draws, sculpture parts); **slow 20–26** (the site's reveal = 26, unfolds, masks, the flood); the portrait's settle is a 45-frame working hold |
| Stagger | 3 frames between siblings (the site's 80–100 ms); 2 frames inside a word build; never more than 15 frames for a group |
| Entrance pattern | **Type rises out of a rule**: each word sits in a slot whose bottom edge is a hairline or a mask, and rises 0.2 em into place while fading in (12 frames, EASE). Components (tags, eyebrows, caption, button) use the site's reveal: 34 px rise (22 px × film scale) and fade over 26 frames. Objects in a well rise from their own ground shadow. No scale-ins, no blur-ins. |
| Rolls | One slot, one shared mask: the outgoing text leaves upward (6 frames, DEPART) while the incoming rises from below (12 frames, EASE), 4 px of vertical motion blur only on the roll. Numerals roll like an odometer: only the digit that changes moves ("01" → "02" rolls the 1). Two texts never overlap. |
| Hover detail | The site's lifts: the button rises 6 px (3 px × 2) on "talk"; images settle from scale 1.06 to 1.0 |
| Camera | One breath for the whole film: the shell group scales 1.000 ↔ 1.012 and drifts ±4 px vertically on a 9 s INOUT cycle (under 8 px/s). It never moves on a transition. The ambient blobs drift with `Ambient drift` 0 → 1 over the film. |
| Marks | One per phrase at most: the selection highlight (once), two strikes, the ? losing its hook. |

Emotion to motion: A (problem) is the only beat where things go off-axis (rows tilt 3–6°, slowly); from P4 on,
everything moves on horizontals and verticals: left to right for progress (uncover, connectors, strikes), upward for
results (bars, the arrow turning ↗), downward for settling (parts landing, the point landing).

  - `RiseFromRule`: a word in an overflow-hidden slot whose bottom edge is the rule; rises 0.2 em with fade.
  - `Roll`: one slot, shared mask, outgoing up / incoming from below; `odometer` mode rolls only changed characters.
  - `RuleLine`: draw (scaleX from left), converge (translate + scaleX), fold/unfold a child (scaleY about the rule).

    size; used for ? → portrait (f201) and portrait → point (f300). Render "?" as two parts so its hook can fade
    separately from its point (clip the glyph above the point, draw the point as a div at the measured position).
  - `Flood`: the dark layer with `clip-path: circle(r at 1360px 665px)`, and the type that sits under it drawn twice
    (light version outside, dark-panel version inside the clip).
