# Duendr: facts (Karl's brief, the screenshots, duendr.app's public landing page; 6 Nov 2026)
Only these and the script may appear on screen.

- Duendr connects creative professionals with businesses, venues, brands and other creatives across Málaga and the Costa del Sol.
- Creatives: showcase a portfolio, share availability, find work, discover people to collaborate with. Creatives join by invitation
  (landing: "Málaga · Creatives by invite · Venues and brands join free"; "I have an invite", "Request an invite").
- Businesses and venues: post opportunities, browse portfolios, find creative talent; they join and post for free.
- Explore opportunities by location (map with city pins and counts, "All cities"), connect, chat about the details, arrange bookings directly.
- No commission: creatives keep their full fee.
- Focus on the quality of work and recommendations from completed bookings.
- Landing headline: "Get booked for your duende, not your follower count." ("duende" in coral italic). Sub: "Creatives post their work. Venues and brands post what they need. You book each other directly."
- App UI (dark): header "duendr" + bell + "All cities"; toggle "Find work / Find talent"; chips "All, Hiring, Cover, Collab, This week, Show distance";
  feed cards (COLLAB tag, title, poster + verified badge, city, role row "Open · 0/1", "Fee to agree · Time to agree", "See all roles", "Connect →");
  "This weekend on the coast" (BOOKED ON DUENDR card with date tile); map with coral pins + bottom sheet ("Marbella · 6 open jobs", rows with fee or "On request",
  "See all 6 in Marbella"); People list (name, verified/new badge, roles, city, fee range "€150–€250", availability "Most days / Weekends");
  Work grid; search "What are you looking for? Find exactly what you need" ("Wedding photographer in Marbella"); new post "What's new?"
  (I'm available / Let's collab / Add new work, "Continue →"), type-of-work chips, "Say I'm free"; "Available" post with RECAP and
  "Who pulled up"; profile (Bookings, Saved, My card, Invite; Work / Posts / About); "Your Duendr card" (share to story); bottom nav Board, Search, Post (+), Messages, Profile.
- Cities for the map: Málaga, Marbella, Estepona, Fuengirola, Torremolinos, Nerja, Sotogrande, Ronda, Sevilla, Granada, Cádiz.
