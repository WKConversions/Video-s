# Context for the K.B SaaS film (11 October 2026)

## Karl's brief (verbatim)
"Now with the better and updated skill. Make a very SaaS-like (style of the new reference videos) video for K.B
(kruslockbosconsultancy.com). Never mention K.B Consultancy in the video, only K.B, don't include the team members in
the video and don't get inspiration from their hero section video. Do the 4% norm." He supplied the ElevenLabs voice-over
(Christina, energetic commercial American female, 37.8 s) and the script below.

## Format and constraints
- 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 39.5–40 s (voice-over placed at +0.50 s; last word ends 38.0 s; a short logo hold after).
- Voice-over is fixed: use the script as written.
- On screen and in sound: only "K.B" (the site writes it "K.B", no final period). Never "Consultancy", never the domain
  (it contains "consultancy"), never the social handles.
- No team members: no photos, names or portraits of K.B people (Robin Bos, Linus Kruslock, Tom van Greevenbroek, Youri de
  Beurs), no group photo. People, if any, are generic (initials or a cursor labelled "Your team").
- The site's hero video: never looked at, never a reference.
- The 4% norm: the median frame should have about 4% of its area changing (scripts/motion_check.py: share of the 160×90
  frame changing by more than 6 levels), reached with motion that MEANS something (content streaming, piling up, playing,
  connecting, typing, scene whips), never with decorative drift and never with text sliding left and right (VoxFlow
  correction). Karl's references read smooth at 0.4–1.1%, so 4% needs content in motion, not more camera.
- K.B-specific corrections (examples/corrections.md): smooth before everything; the camera only breathes; nothing pops past
  its size (no overshoot); nothing flies around; the sign-off is the calmest moment (only the logo and the words come in);
  one thing to watch at a time (an earlier K.B film was "a bit too busy so you can't see what's happening"); readable
  sizes (UI at 2–4× real size, the subject the largest thing on screen).
- Style: "very SaaS-like", in the style of Karl's 11 October references (ref28 Grok Bot, ref29 CrowdReply, ref30 Grok Bot
  Team Bots, ref31 Morphic, ref32 Payy, ref33 Shopify Sidekick): bright white/pale stages, clean product UI at large size
  (prompt bars, chat bubbles, status lines with a shimmer, cards, toggles, cursors that click and cause the next scene),
  one readable line of kinetic type at a time, words popping on whole on their spoken word with one glide, slot swaps,
  pale tint fields per section, exponential arrivals from speed, accelerating shorter exits, cuts on the fastest frame,
  scene changes inside the voice's pauses, no overshoot. Their brand, interfaces, palettes and copy are never copied.
- Earlier K.B films to NOT repeat (other branches): "In, beside, behind" (kb-tech-partner: a footage business tile, tool
  logo conveyors/walls/treadmills, the founders' photo, the K.B tile flipping, a road from Strategy to Implementation,
  a lens from the logotype's period) and the K.B explainer (a copy of a JustCall reference: logo circles docking into an
  app window, a month calendar, an infinity loop, avatars row). Choose new strategies.

## K.B: what the film may say and show (harvest/facts.md, verified live 11 Oct 2026)
- Positioning: "The Embedded Tech Partner for Growing Businesses"; "We build the systems your business runs on, with you";
  "We're the technical team you don't have to hire yet. We build your software and automation with you, then stick around
  to run it." Nav pill "K.B" expands to "K.B Tech Partner" on hover.
- Services: AI, Agents & Automation (Custom AI Agents; No-code automation workflows; Multi-step process pipelines;
  Cross-platform integrations; Connecting tools to eliminate manual work; Use of tools like Make and n8n). Software
  Development (Responsive website development; Mobile app development; Custom business dashboards; Software tailored to
  your operations; Fast and maintainable solutions; Tools designed for both teams and customers).
- "We connect your tools, platforms, and data sources so they operate as a single, coherent system" (e.g. "linking a CRM
  to your support desk, syncing data across platforms, or connecting internal systems to external APIs"); "From data
  routing and approval flows to scheduled tasks and conditional logic, we build systems that run reliably in the
  background"; AI agents "integrate directly into your workflows and support your team across daily operations".
- Process: 01 Diagnose ("We look at how your business actually works today and find where the friction is."), 02 Build
  ("We design and build the system with your team, in the open, so nothing arrives as a surprise."), 03 Run ("We stay on
  to run, maintain and improve what we built as your business grows."). "We build alongside your team from day one".
- After delivery: Hosting (uptime and monitoring), Maintenance (monitoring and alerts when something breaks, regular
  backups, dependency and integration updates, reconnecting APIs when they change), Growth.
- "…turning operational complexity into clarity"; "design tailored workflows, strategies, and tools that simplify
  operations"; "Practical: We build what solves your problem, not what's trendy."
- Tools K.B builds with (real logos in assets): n8n, Make, Claude, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Next.js, TypeScript, Odoo and more.
  FAQ names integrations with Google Workspace, Slack, ClickUp, Notion, Airtable (names only, no logo files).
- CTA copy: "Book a Free Discovery Call", "Let's talk".
- Numbers on the site (Founded 2024, 25 client projects, 100+ automations, 5+ languages) and client cases exist, but the
  film doesn't need figures; never invent one. Client names and logos: not cleared for an ad, leave out.
- Illustrative interfaces are allowed as examples of what K.B builds (a workflow, an agent chat, a dashboard), with
  copy drawn from the lines above; never a claimed K.B product feature that isn't on the site, never fake client data
  presented as real.
