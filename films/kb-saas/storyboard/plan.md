# K.B: "Asked, answered, running" (SaaS film, 40 s): the production plan

Direction: concept B ("the prompt is the narration": the voice acted out by interface text, one upward scroll) with A's
grafts (the workspace built around the business, Diagnose → Build → Run on one card with two cursors, "you" as the
business's pill). Karl asked to go straight to the video (11 Oct), so the judge panel was stopped after concepts A and B;
both are in `concepts/`.

**Thesis:** K.B is the answer to the question every growing business asks. The film is that conversation: the tasks pile up
between your tools, you ask "What if everything just worked together?", K.B answers each need with something running,
and the question rewrites itself into the answer.

**Allowed patterns:** an illustrative request thread between "Your team" and K.B; two generic cursors ("Your team", "K.B");
illustrative examples of what K.B builds (a connection, a workflow, an agent, a dashboard, monitoring), with copy from K.B's
site only; generic tool names (Email, CRM, Spreadsheets, Invoices, Calendar, Chat, Forms, Support desk); K.B's nav pill
("K.B" → "K.B Tech Partner"); one full cyan field. No figures, no client names or logos, no team, never "Consultancy".

**Motion identity:** clean and precise. Signature curve: the exponential arrival (starts at speed, ×0.8 a frame; 90% in
10–12 f, then a tail). Exits: exponential ease-in, half the frames, always upward; scene changes on the fastest frame,
inside the voice's pauses. No overshoot anywhere. One arrival for all text: whole words pop on (no fade, blur or rise)
with a pale cyan wake band behind the newest word; the question is typed, the agent's reply streams. The camera never
moves. Motion for the 4% norm comes from content: the slot roll, the task feed, the typing, capsules syncing, runs
looping, streams, scans, cursors, cascades, the scroll hand-offs and the field masks.
