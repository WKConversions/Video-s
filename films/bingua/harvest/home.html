How it works

Watch

Dual-transcript videos in any of the 6 languages available. Just scroll to the next video to keep learning.

Understand

Tap any word for its translation and explanation.

Save

Tap "+ save" to save a word or sentence to learn later.

Review

Review saved words using flashcards or quizzes.

01

Scroll through videos while learning a new language

No lesson screen, no set of example sentences. Just real videos in a language that you chose, about a topic you are interested in.

Under the video, the transcript keeps pace with the speech, the lines moving as it's spoken. Dual-language subtitles mean that you can easily see the translations. You're not studying in a traditional way, you're watching something and learning at the same time.

02

Don't know a word? Tap it

If a word goes past that you don't know, tap it, and a pop-up opens right where you were already looking: what it means, what kind of word it is, and a plain-English note on what it's doing in that sentence.

When it's the whole sentence confusing you rather than one word, swipe that line sideways. You get a short breakdown of how it fits together, the kind of thing a tutor would lean over and tell you.

No switching apps. No dictionary tab. No losing your place.

03

Saving a word takes one tap

Tap "+". That's it. It gets saved to your cards.

Nothing to name, nothing to file. It's saved, and you never need to leave the app.

This is the part that makes it stick: you never sit down to study. Your card deck fills up on its own, as a side effect of watching things you actually wanted to watch. Every card in it came from a word or sentence you picked.

04

Review words whenever you like

Some time later, there's a number on the Cards tab. The word or sentence you saved is waiting to be reviewed. You say how well you knew it, and Bingua works out when to show it to you next.

Want to push harder? Run a ten-question quiz built entirely from your own cards: listening, typing, matching, saying words out loud into the mic, and more.

Questions

Is there a free version?

No. Bingua is subscription-only: $7.99 a month, or $39.99 a year. The yearly plan comes with a 7-day free trial; the monthly plan doesn't have one. We'd rather tell you that here than after you've downloaded it.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Bingua works completely as a guest, storing your cards and progress on your device, so you can subscribe without ever signing up. An account is optional. It syncs your cards, progress and settings across devices, and it's the only way to carry a subscription to a new phone.

Which languages can I learn?

Six: Spanish, French, German, Dutch, Italian and Portuguese, each with its own library of real video. English is always the translation side. One subscription covers all six, so you can switch whenever you like.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Bingua works for complete beginners too: the platform is built so that the speech in the videos goes at the same speed as your current level in that language. Even if real video still feels hard at first, the speed controls, word and sentence explanations, familiar subjects, and progressive exposure give you an early foothold. Bingua teaches you from day one and keeps delivering value.

Where do the videos come from?

They're made by native speakers, for native speakers. Real video about real subjects, not clips recorded for a language course. Bingua doesn't host or produce any of it. What Bingua adds is the layer on top: the synced dual-language transcript, the tap-for-translation, the sentence breakdowns, and the review deck built from what you saved.

Is Bingua a course?

Not in the traditional sense, and that's exactly the point. There's no rigid syllabus, no forced Unit 1, no placement test, and nothing dictating what you "should" study today. Instead, the feed and the review deck are the method: you watch what genuinely interests you, save the words that stop you, and the deck brings them back right when you need them. If you're looking to learn a language, Bingua offers something freer, more personal, and far more engaging.
