Bingua

Learn Languages by Scrolling.

Scroll to watch the next video. Every video comes with a dual-language transcript. Tap any word you don't know for an instant translation. Swipe a transcript line to see how the whole sentence fits together. Save anything worth remembering and it becomes a flashcard that comes back exactly when you're about to forget it.

🇪🇸 Spanish, 🇫🇷 French, 🇩🇪 German, 🇳🇱 Dutch, 🇮🇹 Italian and 🇵🇹 Portuguese.

HOW IT WORKS
Watch

Dual-transcript videos in any of the 6 languages available. Just scroll to the next video to keep learning.

Understand

Tap any word for its translation and explanation.

Save

Tap "+ save" to save a word or sentence to learn later.

Review

Review saved words using flashcards or quizzes.

01

Scroll through videos while learning a new language

No lesson screen, no set of example sentences. Just real videos in a language that you chose, about a topic you are interested in.

Under the video, the transcript keeps pace with the speech, the lines moving as it's spoken. Dual-language subtitles mean that you can easily see the translations. You're not studying in a traditional way, you're watching something and learning at the same time.

02

Don't know a word? Tap it

If a word goes past that you don't know, tap it, and a pop-up opens right where you were already looking: what it means, what kind of word it is, and a plain-English note on what it's doing in that sentence.

When it's the whole sentence confusing you rather than one word, swipe that line sideways. You get a short breakdown of how it fits together, the kind of thing a tutor would lean over and tell you.

No switching apps. No dictionary tab. No losing your place.

03

Saving a word takes one tap

Tap "+". That's it. It gets saved to your cards.

Nothing to name, nothing to file. It's saved, and you never need to leave the app.

This is the part that makes it stick: you never sit down to study. Your card deck fills up on its own, as a side effect of watching things you actually wanted to watch. Every card in it came from a word or sentence you picked.

04

Review words whenever you like

Some time later, there's a number on the Cards tab. The word or sentence you saved is waiting to be reviewed. You say how well you knew it, and Bingua works out when to show it to you next.

Want to push harder? Run a ten-question quiz built entirely from your own cards: listening, typing, matching, saying words out loud into the mic, and more.

WHY BINGUA

Built for anyone that wants to learn a language without making it feel like a task
Beginner apps have a ceiling

Their sentences are made up. Nobody actually says them. Bingua uses videos made by real speakers of any of the 6 languages.

Raw videos give you no way of translating or reviewing what you learn

No translation on screen, no way to save the words you didn't know, no help at all. Bingua adds exactly that layer.

A dictionary next to the video ruins the video

All that switching back and forth is why the habit dies after a week. Here the translation and explanation opens where you were already looking, and the video keeps playing as soon as you are done reading.

One loop

Watch → understand → save → review is one continuous motion, and the studying happens as a by-product of the watching.

Bingua is not a course, not a grammar app, not a social network, and not a translator.

One subscription. Everything in it.

Bingua is a subscription app. There's no free tier and no ad-supported version: you subscribe, and you get the whole app.
