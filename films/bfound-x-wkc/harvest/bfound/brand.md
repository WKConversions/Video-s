## Fonts
- Display: **Instrument Serif** 400 (regular + italic), Google Font. All h1/h2/h3, step numbers "01-04", prices "FREE"/"€149", testimonials. Hero h1 96px, h2 60px, tight condensed serif look. Lowercase sentence style.
- UI/body: **Inter** 400/500/600/700, Google Font. Nav 14px, buttons 14-16px/500, small UPPERCASE letter-spaced eyebrow labels ("ABOUT", "SERVICES", "HOW IT WORKS").
- Files: fonts/InstrumentSerif-Regular.ttf, fonts/InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf, fonts/Inter-{400,500,600,700}.ttf.

## Shapes
- `--radius: 1rem` (16px), cards up to ~32px radius; buttons and nav fully pill-shaped (radius 9999).

## Tone / motion
Calm, soft, editorial, airy, warm-personal. Lots of white space, pastel washes (cream → lilac → mist), serif headlines with one or two words recoloured in ink blue. Film should move gently: soft fades and rises, blur-to-sharp, floating cards with soft shadows, glassy sheen; no hard cuts or glitch.

## Distinctive devices
1. Instrument Serif headline in near-black with key words switched to ink blue #485e9d ("a founder who **gets** founders.", "a simple, **honest** process.").
2. Ink-blue pill buttons with ↗ and a floating white pill nav.
3. The "b+sparkle" logo mark and the period in "bFound."
4. Pastel cream/lilac/mist gradient washes and frosted glass 3D UI (search bar + navy cursor).
5. Floating white badges with status dot ("● booking now"), uppercase eyebrow pills.
