# Film v2 plan: "Adventures worth talking about", storybook all the way

## Why a v2
Karl's notes on v1: the shadow-theatre animation (the child and parent silhouettes) looked low quality. The client's
own words set the direction: show how something from a child's everyday life ("a child feels left out at school")
becomes an adventure where the child is the hero; end with something parent and child can talk about, in a world they
want to return to; beautiful storybook imagery, the purple-and-gold identity, warm narration, playful motion.

So v2 keeps the same voice-over and drops the shadow theatre entirely. Every image is Tale Forge's own storybook art
(the sample book *Iris och den sparade platsen*, the hero portrait, the adventure cards, the astronaut, the Morgon
aurora, the logo) or a hand-drawn crayon doodle, the way a child would draw their day. New music and new effects.

## Thesis
A small, real moment (left out of a game) is drawn in a child's crayon, tangles up, unwinds into a gold thread, and
the thread leads into a book where the child becomes the hero. The ending is a conversation drawn as speech bubbles.
