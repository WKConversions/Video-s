# Plan and storyboard — Tale Forge "Adventures worth talking about" (EN, 70.0 s)

## Thesis
A small sad moment from today walks into a book and comes out as a conversation. The real world is a **shadow
theatre** (Night-violet silhouettes against lamp-gold light); the story world is **Tale Forge's own watercolour**.
The child's silhouette is cut from Tale Forge's hero portrait, so the book can literally paint the child into the
hero. The night ends in **Morgon**: the site's own Natt → Morgon switch is "tomorrow".

**Allowed patterns:** silhouettes on lamp light; paint blooms (a watercolour blot mask) out of a named object; light
blooms out of the book; Tale Forge's own UI (glass chips, the narrator pill, the two choices, the endings map, the
Natt/Morgon switch); storybook captions in Lora, word by word on the spoken words. **Not allowed:** pans or zooms on
transitions, bounce, glows without a light source, UI that isn't Tale Forge's, any claim not in `harvest/facts.md`.

## Motion identity
- Personality: bedtime calm: things breathe, float and glow; the hand (a touch, a switch) is quick and small.
- Signature curve: the site's own settle `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)` for every arrival; ease-in-out for breaths and loops.
- Duration palette: 0.45 s (UI rise, the site's tfStartIn: 22 px), 0.9 s (path draw, page turn), 1.0–1.6 s (paint and light blooms), 7–9 s floats (the site's float1/float2).
- Entrance pattern: words rise out of a soft blur on their spoken word; pictures bloom out of an object; UI rises 22 px.
- Camera: one slow breath for the whole film (≤ 8 px, ≤ 1.5 %).
