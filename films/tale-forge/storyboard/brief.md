## Assumptions (for Karl)
1. **Style mapping.** Not an intake-form film, so no intake style applies. The look is the client's own: Tale Forge's
   storybook watercolour for the story world, its night/morning (Natt/Morgon) themes, glass UI, Lora + Schibsted Grotesk.
2. **The real world as shadow theatre.** We have no illustration of a child coming home or of a parent and child. Rather
   than invent off-brand art, the real-world moments are a silhouette theatre (in the tradition of Lotte Reiniger's
   fairytale films): Night-violet silhouettes against lamp-gold light. The child's silhouette is cut from Tale Forge's
   own hero portrait of Iris, so "your child is the hero" can paint that silhouette into the hero. If Kevin would rather
   have fully illustrated home scenes, the prompts for them are in `harvest/missing.md`.
3. **Length.** The VO sets the length: 66.6 s of voice + 1.0 s pre-roll + 3.4 s end hold = 71.0 s. To reach 60 s the VO
   would need to lose about 7 s; candidates are in `storyboard/plan.md` (notes).
4. **Music.** "Frost Waltz" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0: free, but a credit line is required. Flagged
   to the client as the brief asks; a licensed track can replace it in the same edit.
5. **Approval.** Karl asked for the finished film directly with VO and sound direction, so the storyboard
   (`storyboard/storyboard.png`) is delivered together with the draft rather than as a stop before the build.
