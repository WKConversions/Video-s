## What Tale Forge is
- Name "Tale Forge" (two words). Tagline in the film: "Adventures worth talking about." [B][S]; site footer: "Tale Forge, storybooks that remember your world." [H]
- Personalised illustrated adventures where children become the hero, explore the pictures and help shape the story [B].
- "A small moment from today can become a personalised illustrated adventure where your child is the hero." [S] (the
  parent story-from-today flow is still being completed [B]; the line is the client's own script).
- The narrator reads aloud, page by page; twice in every book the story stops and the child chooses; the choice changes
  what happens next [H home, step 4]. Two choices make four paths and four endings [H step 5].
- Later books remember the hero, the friends and the places [H step 6, "The world remembers"].
- Themes: Natt (night) and Morgon (morning) [H].
- URL: tale-forge.app [H].
