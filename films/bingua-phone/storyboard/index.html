Bingua · storyboard · 36 s · 1920×1080

Learn languages by scrolling.

Every move in the film is the app doing its job: real videos play, the feed scrolls, subtitles keep pace with the speech, a word is tapped, a sentence is swiped, a word is saved, cards flip and a quiz is answered. The motion comes from those actions, not from drift.

Direction

StageBingua's navy (the icon's #0E1826) with one large phone running the real Bingua interface, rebuilt from the app: XP bar, video, For you / Discover, dual transcript, tab bar with the Cards badge.
FootageFour royalty-free Mixkit clips play inside the phone as the feed: street food (Spanish), an outdoor vlog (French), pasta (Italian), a creator talking to camera (German). Transcript lines are written for them, with translations.
Productive 5 %Motion comes from the real footage, feed swipes, the transcript advancing, taps, pop-ups, cards flipping, the word flying into Cards. The camera only breathes. No idle sliding.
LiftsWhat the finger opens (the word card, the sentence breakdown, the flashcard) also lifts out of the phone at a readable size, to its right.
Words on screenOnly "Bingua" and the closing line "Learn languages by Scrolling." (the site's own). Everything else is the app's own text.

Shots, one per line

0.0–4.6
What if learning a language felt more like scrolling than studying?

The phone shows a flat textbook lesson: a verb table, a grey "Lesson 4 · 25 min" header. On "scrolling" a thumb flicks it away and the feed scrolls in fast, landing on a real video that starts playing. On "studying" the lesson page falls away off the frame.

4.6–5.8
Meet Bingua.

The phone becomes the logo: its video turns into the play triangle, two transcript lines into the two bars, the frame into the navy tile. "Bingua" sets beside it.

Bingua
5.8–8.9
Watch real videos in the language you want to learn.

The tile opens back into the phone. Swipe: Spanish street food, then French vlog, then Italian pasta; each video plays with its language chip (ES, FR, IT) as it lands.

8.9–11.6
Follow along with dual-language subtitles.

Back to the Spanish video. Under it the transcript keeps pace: each line lights blue as it is spoken, word by word, the English line under it. The live line lifts out large beside the phone.

11.6–14.9
Don't know a word? Just tap it for an instant translation.

"famoso" pulses as unknown; the finger taps it; the word card opens in place (famoso · famous · ADJECTIVE) and lifts out large.

14.9–18.9
Need more context? Swipe to understand the entire sentence.

The finger swipes the transcript line sideways; the sentence breakdown slides up: "¿Qué está pasando aquí?" · What's going on here? · EXPLANATION 1, 2, building line by line.

18.9–21.8
Found something useful? Save it in one tap.

"+ Save" is tapped, turns to "✓ Saved"; the word flies down into the Cards tab and its badge goes 14 → 15.

21.8–24.8
Then practice with smart flashcards and quizzes.

Cards tab: the "famoso" flashcard flips to its answer and is marked "Got it"; a quiz question follows, "famous" is picked and turns green, +10 XP.

24.8–27.6
No boring lessons. No complicated routines.

The textbook lesson card comes back and is flicked away; a 7-step study-routine checklist folds up and is flicked away.

27.6–31.4
Just videos you love, and a language you're learning.

Feed again: a video is hearted; the XP bar fills to the daily goal and the streak flame goes 2 → 3.

31.4–36.0
Bingua. Learn languages by scrolling.

The phone becomes the logo again; "Bingua", then "Learn languages by Scrolling." with "Scrolling." in cyan, and the App Store badge. The camera settles.
