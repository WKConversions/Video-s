Notes

  • The voice-over is the script as written; nothing was rewritten. "K.B." is spoken; the screen always writes "K.B" as the site does.
  • No URL on the end card: the domain contains "consultancy". The end card uses the site's own words: "We grow with you." (About) and "Book a Free Discovery Call" (home).
  • "Your business" is a royalty-free Mixkit clip (4809, a team at a table seen from above, no faces to camera, no brand). It is never presented as a K.B client or the K.B team. The founders' photo is the site's own About photo.
  • The three ringed parts are named with the manual work K.B's own client cases describe (Excel files, CRM updates, follow-ups); the solution tools are the ones the site lists for AI and automation (Make, n8n, Claude). No figures, no client names, no case on screen.
  • "Strategy" is only loosely supported by the site now (it leads with building and running); the road shows it as a plan turning into the built business.
  • Nothing from the site's hero video (or its other videos) was downloaded, sampled or used.

## The arc (storytelling.md)
- **Spine:** K.B is the tech partner that stays: it understands, builds and launches with you, so technology finally
  helps the business grow.
- **Hook (0–4.6 s):** growth that technology should lift and instead holds back: a promise and its betrayal in one
  object, in the first second.
- **Problem (4.6–9.3 s):** the search: too many real tools, no starting point, a knot.
- **Turn (9.4 s):** K.B comes in, literally, and the knot resolves around it. The film's one full brand-colour field.
- **Mechanism (11.3–23 s):** advisor vs partner, then the four steps acted out on the viewer's business, each on its
  verb.
- **Benefit (23–26.6 s):** the whole path, side by side.
- **Resolution (26.6–34 s):** the callback: technology alone stays flat; with the partner behind it, growth moves.
  The end card grows out of that partner.
- **Energy curve:** high open (hook) → cramped (problem, tension) → release (turn, the peak) → driving and even
  (process, four quick steps, the densest stretch) → warm (side by side) → a held breath ("alone", the emptiest
  frame) → resolve (lift, logo, calm end card).

## Setups and payoffs
- The business's growth (hook) is held back (1), resumes when the solutions go live (5), and rises for good when the
  partner is behind it (8).
- The real tools of the problem beat (2) return as "technology alone" (7).
- K.B arrives "in" (3), stands beside (4, 6) and ends behind (8): in, beside, behind, three positions of one object.

Thesis and allowed patterns
# Thesis

**Thesis:** K.B is the one tile that isn't another tool: it comes *in*, stands *beside* your business and ends up
*behind* it, so the business that technology held back grows. The film is told with K.B's own shapes (the navy logo
tile, the site's tool tiles, the cyan accent, the period between K and B), one real-looking business at work, and the
real people of K.B.

**Allowed patterns:**
- The site's real tool tiles ("Tools we build with", their real logos on navy) as "technology": a floor, a reel, a
  wall, a treadmill. Never recoloured; on the grip they turn their blank navy backs.

## What "5% motion" means here, and how it is reached
`motion_check.py` measures, at 160×90, the share of the frame whose brightness changes by more than 6 levels from one
frame to the next; the film's median frame must reach **5.0%** (K.B v3, approved: 1.7%; Karl's 25 references: median
3.5%, range 0.5–6.6%). Karl's K.B lessons still hold: the 3% target of October was first reached with camera moves and
those read as spikes, so **the camera only breathes** and the motion comes from the content: big subjects that keep
travelling, colour fields that grow out of objects, real photographs that keep moving inside their frames, and a second
layer working inside every scene, without crowding the frame (the probe's BUSY and `busy_check.py` stay clean).
