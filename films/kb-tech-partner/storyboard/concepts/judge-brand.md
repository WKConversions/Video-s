## Winner: C. Fixes

1. **Drop "Strategy" and "Implementation" (16-17).** They are subtitles, and facts.md notes the site no longer leads with strategy. Let the bar ride show it.
2. **Make the end card all brand.**
   - BT exits or freezes by 32.5 s. KT stands whole and unobstructed: no logotype peeking above footage, and no stock face larger than the logo.
   - Set "K.B TECH PARTNER" in Copperplate caps, as on the nav hover. Do the same on 09, where C uses Montserrat.
   - The button is the site's cyan pill with dark text.
3. **Third-party logos are never the villain.**
   - The grip (03) and the treadmill (19-20) show the tiles' blank navy backs.
   - Logos face up only on 01 and when K.B puts them to work (14, a flip already in C's grammar).
   - Never recolour a logo.
4. **Variety budget.** Tile-on-tile runs through about 80% of the phrases.
   - On 12-13 open BT full-bleed, its corners straightening like the field on 07.
   - Make "alone." (20) a full-frame type moment with the floor cropped.
5. **Leave a sliver of KT at BT's edge on 19-20.** Fully hidden, it reads as K.B leaving. The sliver sets up "behind".
6. **Choose a clip from K.B's world.** A small team doing admin or intake at laptops, closer to the real cases (recruitment, hospitality). The café passes only if no one faces the camera and no brand shows. Cut P06's slip.
