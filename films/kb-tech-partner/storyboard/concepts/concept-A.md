# Concept A: Grow, Unboxed

**Thesis:** Technology that should grow your business boxes it in; K.B steps into that same business, looks under its
spreadsheet, turns its manual chores into running automations and stays beside it, so the held-back growth moves.

**Allowed patterns:** the example sheet "your business.xlsx" (columns Bookings / Invoices / Follow-ups / CRM updates,
chores named on K.B's case pages; navy skeleton rows, no names or figures; "To do"/"Done" pills); four example file
windows; real tool logos from K.B's tools strip; a dotted node flow in the style of the Make scenarios K.B builds; one
report page titled "Advice"; one Mixkit clip as "your business"; the founders' real group photo; the site's lockup
"K.B Tech Partner", line "we grow with you" and CTA "Book a Free Discovery Call". No URL, no "Consultancy".

## Art direction

- **World:** one light-grey table (#F7F7F7), white surfaces with soft wide shadows, the sheet on a slight 2.5D tilt.
  Framing changes only through objects (a cell unrolls into a grid, a tile grows into a field, a lens opens), never a zoom.
- **Colour logic:** grey = stuck, manual (boxed "grow", "To do", the pointer). Navy #293A51 = K.B and the business's
  ink (tiles, type, skeleton bars), dark on purpose so moving content registers. Cyan #38C8FF = K.B's touch (ring,
  pulse, lit nodes, "Done", the freed "grow"). **The one navy field:** 10.3-13.3 s, grown out of the K.B tile and
  folded back into it. A pale-cyan tint grows out of the last node on "life" and lies under the running flow.
- **Type:** Montserrat ExtraBold 96-160 px kinetic lines, Bold 64-80 px labels, SemiBold 40 px UI; Copperplate only for "K.B".
- **Mixed media, decided once:** real photo = the founders' group photo (P18 only). Footage = one Mixkit clip of a small
  business at work (hands, paperwork, laptop, no face to camera), seen only inside the lens (P11-13). Hero video: never.
- **Threads:** (1) **"grow"**: rises (P02), boxed in a cell (P03), seeds the sheet, still boxed at the callback (P19),
  freed with K.B behind it (P21), lands in "We grow with you." (2) **The K.B tile**: comes in (P07), docks inside the
  sheet (P09), becomes the lens (P10-13), travels the path (P17), opens into the team (P18), stands behind "grow"
  (P21): in, inside, beside, behind. (3) **Tool tiles**: conveyor (P01), endless dropdown (P04), four nodes (P14),
  conveyor past the boxed word (P19).
- **Motion personality:** clean, unhurried, always travelling. Conveyors B10 (90-150 px/s) on a 15-18° rising
  diagonal; signature curve B3 (soft start, long settle) for arrivals; B2 with mid-move blur for big moves; no
  overshoot, pops or loops. Entrances come from the frame edge or out of an object already on screen.
- **Transitions:** (a) mask or field out of an object (cell → grid, tile ↔ navy field, lens iris, tile → photo);
  (b) layout transformation (columns fold into nodes, sheet folds into the lane); (c) open on the centre line (P19).
- **Camera:** one breath, ≤1.5% scale over 30 s, ≤6 px/s drift: under the metric's threshold, so all counted motion is content.

## Reaching 5% without the camera or busy frames

A 160×90 pixel counts when it shifts >6 levels. A sharp navy-on-grey edge (contrast ~190) does that above ~12 px/s;
cyan (~85) above ~26 px/s; light-grey skeleton bars (~25) almost never. So the budget is **the length of dark edges in
motion**, not speed: moving content is inked navy, conveyors run diagonally so both edge directions count, and the
sheet never stops scrolling until it folds (P14).

| Beat | Big moving content | Est. changed |
|---|---|---|
| Hook 0-4.7 | 6-7 diagonal navy tiles (4 edges + logo ≈130 low-res px each), word arrivals; cell unroll | 5.5-7% (15% unroll) |
| Problem 4.7-9.3 | sheet scroll (≈120 px per navy row × 9), dropdown list, window fan, drags | 8-11% |
| Turn 9.4-11.3 | tile crossing, field growing out (annulus ≈2,000 px) | 7-14% |
| Advice 11.3-13.0 | logo glide, report arrival, navy-banded pages riffling | 4-6% |
| Partner 13.1-15.0 | field folding back, grid shifting right, rows | 9-14% |
| Steps 15.1-21.1 | rows, lens travel, footage, column collapse (≈20%) | 8-12% |
| Flow 21.2-26.5 | record cards with navy bars on the diagonal (≈200 px each × 5), dashes, tile travel, photo card | 6-9% |
| Callback 26.6-29.8 | parting (≈20%), then the conveyor | 5.5-7% |
| Close 29.8-34 | tile rising, "grow" rising, calm end card | 1-4% |

**Riskiest:** 10.9-11.4 (navy, logo alone: glide starts 11.0, report arrival 11.25); 11.3-12.1 (report on navy: bigger
page, navy band, darker lines); 30.6-31.1 (last tile leaving overlaps the K.B tile rising); the end card (calm by rule).
≈3.3 s below 5% in all, so the median sits near 6.5% if the middle beats hold. Measure each beat on the draft; levers:
darker ink on the moving layer, steeper diagonal, sheet scroll 45 → 90 px/s.

## Risks, cuts, assets

- **Showreel risk** (many devices). Cut first: the riffle, the photo riding the line (dock it), the logos lighting in P19.
- **Callback leans on memory:** the cell keeps its exact look, the conveyor its diagonal and tiles.
- **Busy under the scroll:** sheet down to 45 px/s while a foreground object moves (navy ink still counts).
- **Footage** must read as a small business, never a K.B client; fallback a photo panning in the lens ≥90 px/s.
- **Not Excel:** no Microsoft green or ribbon; a generic grid.
- **On disk:** film/public/img/kb-logo-tile.svg, kb-wordmark.svg; film/public/fonts; harvest/ref-images/about-team-photo.jpg;
  Make scenarios in harvest/ref-images as node-style reference only.
