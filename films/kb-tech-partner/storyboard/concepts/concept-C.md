# Concept C: In, Beside, Behind

## 1. Thesis

**Thesis:** K.B is the one tile that isn't another tool. It comes in, stands beside your business and ends up behind it, so the business that technology held back grows. The whole film is told with K.B's own shapes: the navy logo tile, the site's tool tiles, the cyan accent and the period between K and B.

**Allowed patterns:**
- The site's real tool tiles as "technology" (floor and conveyor).
- One Mixkit clip in a rounded tile as "your business": the viewer's business, never a client or the team.
- A white note reading "Advice".
- A cyan dot drawn out of the logotype's period.
- The business tile cut into nine pieces as an exploded view.
- Make, n8n and Claude as the solution tiles (from the site's "Tools we use").
- The K.B tile stretched into a bar labelled "Strategy" and "Implementation".
- The founders' Málaga photo on the back of the K.B tile.
- The site's "Tech Partner" lockup, "We grow with you." and "Book a Free Discovery Call".

There is no URL, no "Consultancy", no figures and no hero video.

## 2. Art direction

- **World:**
  - One canvas, #F7F7F7, with soft wide navy shadows.
  - Every shape is the site's rounded square (radius about 14% of its side).
- **Cast:**
  - **KT**, the K.B tile: the favicon gradient #354C67→#3D5574 with a white Copperplate "K.B".
  - **BT**, the business tile: footage in a rounded square.
  - **Tool tiles:** #293A51, each with a real logo.
  - **Dot:** cyan #38C8FF.
- **Colour logic:**
  - Navy = technology and K.B. Many separate tiles = many tools; one unbroken field = one partner.
  - Cyan only marks things (ring, cuts, underline, dot); it is never used for body text.
  - Grey footage = held back; colour = alive.
  - **The one full navy field** comes at 10.3–11.3 s: KT grows over the whole tool floor on "comes in", then folds back into the tile.
- **Type:**
  - Montserrat ExtraBold 180–200 px for "grow" and "alone.".
  - Montserrat Bold 64–110 px for the other words.
  - Copperplate only for "K.B".
  - Words appear on 8 of the 21 phrases.
- **Media, decided once:**
  - One Mixkit clip in HD: 6161 "Cafe owner discussing business" (owner and staff over a laptop, tablet and notes; the camera drifts). Backup: 48713.
  - The founders' group photo, on P18 only.
  - The real tool logos.
  - No case images, no UI and no client logos: the geometry carries the film.
- **Threads:**
  - **KT:** comes *in* (P07) → becomes the field → crosses the gap to stand *beside* (P09) → stretches into a road (P16–17) → turns over into the team *beside* you (P18) → hides *behind* (P19–20) → rises *behind* and lifts (P21).
  - **BT:** grows (P02) → is held down and greyed (P03) → is opened, fixed, closed and coloured (P11–15) → is carried (P17) → is lifted higher than in the hook (P21).
  - **Tool floor:** conveyor (P01–06) → wiped by the field (P07) → returns as three solution tiles inside the business (P14) → returns as a treadmill going nowhere (P19–20) → drops away (P21).
  - **Dot:** leaves the period (P10), does the four steps, goes home (P15).
- **Motion personality:** heavy, sure objects.
  - **Signature curve:** B3 (soft start, long settle).
  - Carries use B2; the conveyor uses B10 at 130 px/s; the dot and ring use B4; flips use B1.
  - No overshoot and no scale-from-zero.
  - **Entrance pattern:** everything slides in from a frame edge or out of another object.
- **Transition families:**
  1. **Flip:** a tile turns over into the next thing.
  2. **Field from a tile:** grow, contract, stretch, gather.
  3. **Split and close:** the business is cut into nine and closed again; the floor arrives and drops away.

## 4. Reaching a 5% median without the camera

I simulated `motion_check.py`'s sampling on the real assets. Its INTER_LINEAR downscale reads about four pixels per 12 px cell, so **flat fields barely count and moving texture does**. These numbers use the 360p previews; HD should read higher.

| Moving content | Frame changed |
|---|---|
| Flat navy 800 px square at 30 / 150 / 400 px/s | 0.0 / 0.5 / 1.4% |
| Type at 170 px, moving 40–120 px/s | 1.0–1.5% |
| Tool floor, 2 rows × 240 px tiles, at 60 / 110 / 150 px/s | 3.1 / 4.6 / 5.1% |
| BT footage at 640 px, still / panning 50 px/s | 1.0 / 2.4% |
| BT at 760 / 880 px, panning 50–60 px/s | 3.6 / 5.3% |
| Team photo in a 900 px tile, panning 20 / 40 / 60 px/s | 2.1 / 5.0 / 7.4% |
| Floor at 110 px/s + BT 640 panning (the hook) | 6.0% |

So the fields carry the meaning, and **the textured surfaces carry the motion**. In every phrase at least 35% of the frame is texture (logos, footage or photo) moving at 40–130 px/s inside or with its frame. The camera keeps v3's breath, and the estimates don't count it.

| Beat | Main moving content | Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| P01–03 | floor + BT panning, lifting and sinking | 6–8% |
| P04–06 | floor (under 45% of the frame) + BT 600 | 5–6% |
| P07 | KT slide + floor, then the field's growing edge | 5%, then about 0.5% for 0.4 s |
| P08–09 | BT 820 panning + field fold, note, KT crossing | 4.5–5.5% (**riskiest**) |
| P10–15 | BT 880 rescaling, nine drifting pieces, flips | 5–8% |
| P16–18 | BT travelling 1000 px; two 620 px tiles panning | 6–10% |
| P19–21 | floor + BT 720, floor exit, the lift | 5–7% |

About 24 of the 32 measured seconds are at 5% or above, so I expect a median of **5.5–6%**.

How the risky holds stay up:
- **P08–09:** BT goes to 880 px with a 60 px/s pan, and drifts towards KT as KT crosses.
- **P07:** the flat field holds for only 0.4 s.

Run `--profile` on the first draft. Pick the clip by its measured HD motion.

## 6. Risks, cuts, assets

**Risks:**
- **Motion depends on texture.** If the HD clip is calm, enlarge BT and pan it faster. Never move the camera to make up the difference.
- **The exploded view** may read as breaking the business. Keep the gaps small. If it doesn't read in a second, fall back to the dot as a lens.
- **KT hidden in P19–20** could read as K.B leaving. It is meant as "technology alone", and the payoff comes 1.5 s later.
- **Logotype in KT's top band at the end.** Ask Karl. Fallback: KT offset up-left with the logotype centred.
- **P06's slip** could feel slapstick.

**Cut first:** P06's slip (BT just slides back), then "grow" sinking (P03), then the dot going home (P15), then the P09 underline.
