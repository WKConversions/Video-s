## The arc (storytelling.md)
- **Spine:** K.B is the tech partner that stays: it understands, builds and launches with you, so technology finally
  helps the business grow.
- **Hook (0–4.6 s):** growth that technology should lift and instead holds back: a promise and its betrayal in one
  object, in the first second.
- **Problem (4.6–9.3 s):** the search: too many real tools, no starting point, a knot.
- **Turn (9.4 s):** K.B comes in, literally, and the knot resolves around it. The film's one full brand-colour field.
- **Mechanism (11.3–23 s):** advisor vs partner, then the four steps acted out on the viewer's business, each on its
  verb.
- **Benefit (23–26.6 s):** the whole path, side by side.
- **Resolution (26.6–34 s):** the callback: technology alone stays flat; with the partner behind it, growth moves.
  The end card grows out of that partner.
- **Energy curve:** high open (hook) → cramped (problem, tension) → release (turn, the peak) → driving and even
  (process, four quick steps, the densest stretch) → warm (side by side) → a held breath ("alone", the emptiest
  frame) → resolve (lift, logo, calm end card).

## Setups and payoffs
- The business's growth (hook) is held back (1), resumes when the solutions go live (5), and rises for good when the
  partner is behind it (8).
- The real tools of the problem beat (2) return as "technology alone" (7).
- K.B arrives "in" (3), stands beside (4, 6) and ends behind (8): in, beside, behind, three positions of one object.
