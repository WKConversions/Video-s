# Thesis

**Thesis:** K.B is the one tile that isn't another tool: it comes *in*, stands *beside* your business and ends up
*behind* it, so the business that technology held back grows. The film is told with K.B's own shapes (the navy logo
tile, the site's tool tiles, the cyan accent, the period between K and B), one real-looking business at work, and the
real people of K.B.

**Allowed patterns:**
- The site's real tool tiles ("Tools we build with", their real logos on navy) as "technology": a floor, a reel, a
  wall, a treadmill. Never recoloured; on the grip they turn their blank navy backs.
- One royalty-free clip (Mixkit 4809, a team at a table seen from above, no faces to camera, no brand) in a rounded
  tile as "your business": the viewer's business, never presented as a K.B client or the K.B team. Grey while held
  back, colour from "bring them to life".
- A white note reading "Advice".
- A cyan dot drawn out of the logotype's period; it becomes a lens.
- The business opened into a 3×3 exploded view ("a visual workflow breakdown", [S]); three parts ringed with K.B's
  own words for manual work: "Follow-ups", "CRM updates", "Excel files" ([C1], [C2]).
- Make, n8n and Claude as the solution tiles (the site's AI & automation tools, [SA], [H]), joined by a dotted flow.
- A road labelled "Strategy" and "Implementation" (diagram ends), the business drawn as a dashed plan at the start
  and built by the end.
- The founders' Málaga photo (the site's About photo) on the back of the K.B tile.
- The site's own words: "Tech Partner" (the nav lockup), "We grow with you." (About H1), "Book a Free Discovery Call".

Nothing else: no URL (the domain says "consultancy"), no "Consultancy", no figures, no client names, no hero video.
