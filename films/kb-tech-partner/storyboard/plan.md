# Production plan: K.B, "In, Beside, Behind" (34.0 s)

PURPOSE: a website and LinkedIn film that positions K.B as the embedded tech partner · AUDIENCE: founders and managers
of startups and SMEs · FORMAT: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps · DURATION: 34.0 s (1020 frames) · CORE MESSAGE: technology
alone doesn't grow a business; K.B comes in, stays beside you and ends up behind your growth · TONE: energetic voice,
clean motion · STYLE AND BRAND: K.B's own site (canvas #F7F7F7, navy #293A51, cyan #38C8FF as marks only, Montserrat,
Copperplate CC for "K.B") · ART DIRECTION: one bright stage of rounded squares; one business at work (footage in a
tile), one K.B tile, the real tool tiles; mixed media decided once: one clip for "your business", the founders' photo
for the partner lines, real tool logos; no UI templates, no invented data · MOTION PERSONALITY: precise premium at the
energetic end: heavy, sure objects, no overshoot, no bounce · SIGNATURE CURVE: ARRIVE `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`
(≈80%), MOVE `(.65,0,.35,1)` for objects already on screen, DEPART for exits; B2 long S for the carries, B10 for the
conveyors, B4 for the dot · DURATION PALETTE: quick 7, standard 13, slow 24 frames · ENTRANCE PATTERN: from a frame
edge or out of another object, never from nothing · TRANSITION FAMILY: (1) a tile flips into the next thing, (2) a
field or a mask grows out of a tile, (3) split and close (the business opens into parts and closes again) · CAMERA:
one slow breath for the film (K.B v3), easing to rest before the sign-off · VOICE-OVER AND MUSIC: ElevenLabs
"Christina", at 0.40 s; Mixkit "Raising Me Higher" (110 BPM), opening up on "That's where K.B comes in".

**The 5% rule of this film** (measured: 5.6% on the draft). The fields and tiles carry the meaning; the textured surfaces carry the motion. In
every phrase a textured surface (the footage, the founders' photo, the logo floor) of at least a third of the frame
keeps travelling at 40–130 px/s inside or with its frame. The camera never makes up the difference.
