# Connecting Booking and CRM Data for a Complete Customer Journey
By integrating AQQO with Pipedrive, Social Impact Factory gained a centralized view of customer activity, booking history, and lead sources, without manually transferring information between systems.
*[image: "aqqo booking reservation system" — /api/horizontal-media/file/aqqo-idihpshu2a-0.png]*
## The Challenge
Social Impact Factory managed coworking spaces, meeting rooms, memberships, and event bookings through AQQO while implementing Pipedrive as its new CRM. Because the two platforms operated independently, customer and booking data remained fragmented.
## Our Approach
We designed and implement an API-based integration between AQQO and Pipedrive:
Integration architecture: designed the complete data flow between the booking platform and CRM
API integration: connected AQQO and Pipedrive through their respective APIs
Automation: built Make.com workflows that retrieve, process, and synchronize booking and customer data
Data mapping: mapped relevant booking details, customer information, and booking sources to the correct Pipedrive fields
Record management: automatically created new CRM records or updated existing customer information
Scalable foundation: developed an architecture that can support additional CRM and customer journey automations
