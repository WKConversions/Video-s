# What We've Built
A look at the ventures we've built and backed, some as equity partners, some fully our own.
Fully Owned Project
## hirebetter.io
An AI recruitment platform that takes the manual work out of hiring: job descriptions, candidate summaries and interview questions in one workspace. Built and owned by K.B.
Equity Partnership
## Tale Forge
Personalised illustrated children's books where the child is the main character and their choices branch the story. Characters and places carry over from one book to the next.
Equity Partnership
## MingelBingo
A digital mingle game for company events. Guests scan QR codes and complete challenges while they talk to each other, which breaks the ice without anyone having to force it.
## Building something you want K.B to back?
We take equity selectively, in ideas we believe in and want to help build. This is not a service you buy, so tell us what you are making and what you need from us.
