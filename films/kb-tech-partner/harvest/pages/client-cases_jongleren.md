# Automating Candidate Intake for Faster Placements
By automating intake and admin work, JongLeren freed recruiters to focus on what matters most: matching the right students with the right internships.
*[image: "Google sheets" — /api/horizontal-media/file/google-sheets.jpg]*
## The Challenge
JongLeren's intake process was draining recruiter time and slowing down placements. Each candidate journey required multiple touchpoints, a 40-minute intake call, and another 30 minutes of manual CRM updates. Recruiters often discovered halfway through that a candidate wasn't even eligible, creating frustration, lost time, and inconsistent data.
## Our Approach
We redesigned the intake workflow from start to finish:
Process audit to identify inefficiencies
Automation: online candidate forms with instant eligibility feedback, AI transcription of intake calls, CRM auto-updates, and auto-generated candidate PDFs
Training & onboarding so the team could confidently adopt the new system
Continuous optimization to refine the workflow
