# Intelligence platform: facts (Karl's explanation + his 36 screenshots, 7 Nov 2026)
Only these and the script may appear on screen.

- A platform WKConversions builds and sells to companies. In a client meeting WKC asks the right questions so the transcript
  captures what matters; their model turns that transcript into a custom platform for the client: a knowledge base per department.
- Departments in the example: Sales, Marketing, Operations, Customer Success, Finance, People (six). Each has three layers:
  Layer 1 "Strategy" (always exactly one document: the department's strategy), Layer 2 "How we work" (e.g. Sales: 2.1 Purpose and
  philosophy, 2.2 Client types and ICP, 2.3 Channels and partners, 2.4 Sales process, 2.5 Service mix, 2.6 Pricing, 2.7 Revenue
  goal and metrics, 2.8 Tools and ownership), Layer 3 "Playbooks" (many documents).
- Home: "Good morning, <name>" / "What would you like to work on?" / cards Knowledge base ("Read how <co> works, from strategy to
  day-to-day playbooks."), Offer ("Paste a call transcript and get a ready offer in minutes."), Strategy ("Tell the agent what
  should change, everything else follows.").
- Knowledge base: "Everything about how <co> works. Read-only — it follows your strategy." Search; "Add transcript".
- Strategy: "Your strategy is the source of truth. Change it here and the rest of the knowledge base follows." Per department the
  strategy document with contents, "Edit text yourself", and the Strategy agent: "What do you want to change?", "Talk normally —
  e.g. 'We now also work with e-commerce brands'", attach a transcript, Send. Flow (screens 24-36): "Raise our minimum project
  price to €450" → "Here's what I took from it" + the suggested change (Approve / Reject) → "Pricing — strategy updated · Updating
  15 related documents" → "4 related documents updated — check them before they go live" (Approve all / Review one by one) →
  "Review updated documents" (diffs; To check / Approved / No change needed) → "All changes" log → the Pricing document shows €450.
- Offer: "Paste a call transcript. I'll pull out what matters, then write an offer from your own prices and process." Steps:
  1 Add transcript, 2 Check what I took (client, decision maker, needs, problems, budget signals, timeline, fit, risks;
  "What do you want to do with this?": Make an offer / Update the knowledge base / Both), 3 Your offer ("Your offer is ready",
  Copy / Download .md / Print / PDF; "What we heard", scope, prices, payment terms, next step). "Earlier offers".
- Settings: profile, the Claude agent (API key, workspace ID, model), Branding (company name, short name, logos, fonts, accent
  colour): the platform is white-label.
