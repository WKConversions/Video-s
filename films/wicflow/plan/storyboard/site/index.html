The work, in blocksv2

A company’s real work (quotes going quiet, enquiries piling up, hours typed in twice) becomes its own shape in Wicflow’s block world, where off-the-shelf software won’t sit. The AI mapping lays the processes out, watches the time drain from each, and finds the highest return. The costed plan ranks it first, lies down as the ground, and its top row rises as the periwinkle first block, guaranteed. Inside that block the quote follow-up runs on its own. Block by block the system builds, each block with its automation running, until all of it is connected. Then it shrinks above the wicflow lockup and unbuilds back into the work, so the film loops.

What changed in v2

Karl on v1: “I like it, but it’s too much text. I want it more SaaS-like: text here and there, but the majority visualising what the voice-over says, or what the platform literally is. Also, give me 1 image with the whole storyboard.”

v2 keeps v1’s story, timing (37.6 s, ten beats) and block-world thread. It answers Karl’s note “too much text” by moving every word into the voice-over except three text moments, and by making each beat show its VO line literally, or show the platform itself, big and centred. The 3D block beats (B2, B5, B7, B8) alternate with large flat UI beats (B1, B3, B4, B6), and one element still becomes the next: cards → block faces → mapping tiles → plan rows → the ground → the first block → its automation → the world → the lockup → the cards.

The rules every beat follows.

Look, from wicflow.com’s CSS and references 1–4

One still radial background, centred (960,540), #131C3A → #0E1630 (45%) → #0B1220, with 2% static dither; it never moves. Text #F3F5FA; muted text rgba(223,230,243,.72); lines rgba(255,255,255,.10). The work cards are opaque navy glass (#161F3F, 1 px rgba(255,255,255,.14), radius 26); the UI windows are glass (rgba(255,255,255,.045–.05), radius 32, even shadow). Periwinkle #6172F3 → #B9C1FF is Wicflow’s colour: return bars, highlights and the first block. Peach (#ffd9c8 → #f3a585, the site’s accent-word gradient) is time: the mapping’s drains and the quiet quote’s ring. Orange #FF5A1F appears only for problems: unread enquiries and the misfit slab. Green #16B857 appears only on ‘Running’. Icons are 2.5 px round-cap line icons in a 48-unit box.

Block rules

Orthographic 2:1 isometric, now centred: a lattice point (i,j,h) sits at (960 + 120(i−j), 554 + 69.5(i+j) − 128h); the silhouette spans x 600–1320, y 170–971 (74% of the frame height). Fourteen blocks as in the reference: 01 Quote follow-ups (0,2,0), the first block; 02 Enquiries answered (2,2,0); 03 New customers (2,0,1); 04 Hours to invoices (0,1,1); 05 Owner’s weekly report (1,0,1); 06 One system (0,0,2). States: WIRE (dashed 6/6, marching 12 px/s), GLASS, SOLID (#F2F3FC/#DCDFF5/#C2C7EC with its number), GLASS-PERI and PERI (#CDD3FF/#B9C1FF/#98A2F5), only ever for 01. Card ↔ block: QUOTE → 01, ENQUIRY → 02, HOURS → 04, REPORT → 05. The mapping’s tiles come from the five named blocks’ top faces.

Type and layout

Outside the UI only three text moments: the two leader labels (Fragment Mono 32, sentence case, a 1.2 px rgba(223,230,243,.5) line and a 9 px dot, as in the references) and the end lockup (the mark, ‘wicflow’ in Manrope 800 92 px as the site’s nav sets it, the line in Stack Sans Headline 500 60 px, the button in Inter 600 38 px). UI text: Inter 500 34–36 for a panel title or row label, Fragment Mono 20–26 for chips, tags and the week track. Everything essential sits inside x 96–1824, y 54–1026, and the subject is at least half the frame. For Finnish, the UI labels run 25–35% longer: the plan rows have room to 560 px, and the lockup line reflows to two lines at 60 px.

Motion identity

calm, clean, Apple. Arrivals decelerate (ARRIVE cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)); exits accelerate (ease-in, 0.2–0.3 s); landings end in a 2–4 px drift with no overshoot (B4 spring, damping 200). No bounce, elastic, anticipation or camera pans. The scale moves are the B1 focus pull, the two zoom-throughs of block 01 (16 frames each way) and the B7 and B9 eases. A new visual lands every 0.5–0.8 s; holds stay alive (drift, marching dashes, data dots, pulses). Motion norm: the house 7% median (motion_check.py at 60 fps); test B2 and B5, the thinnest wire beats, first at the animatic. Sound: effects lead over a quiet music bed (louder with no VO), from films/alumni/sound/lib: soft ticks as the enquiries pile up, keyboard taps for the re-typing, a dull thud and a ‘wrong’ tick on the slab, droplet ticks for the time drains, a lock-on click on the found tile, a card-shuffle swish on the re-rank, a deep whoosh and a plastic ‘thock’ on the tilt, typing ticks and a soft ding on ‘Draft ready’, a pen scratch on each signature, rising clicks block by block, an arpeggio as the studs light, a resolving chord under the lockup, and a reverse shimmer into the loop.

Truth, exclusions and loop

Every on-screen phrase comes from wicflow.com: ‘First block · guaranteed’, ‘All of it connected’, ‘Let’s find the first thing to build’, ‘Book an AI mapping’, ‘AI mapping’, ‘Week 1’ / ‘Week 2’, ‘costed plan’, the five Kallio Lämpö block names, ‘First block’, ‘Quote follow-ups’ and ‘Drafts ready for the salesperson’ (as the tag ‘Draft ready’). There are no figures on screen: the bars and columns are schematic, and every card is placeholder bars and icons. Kallio Lämpö is the site’s made-up heating company; the plan shows its blocks. The VO uses the site’s facts (‘first block, guaranteed’, ‘if it doesn’t do what we agreed, you don’t pay’, ‘no open quote goes quiet’, ‘every block priced in writing before we start’, ‘all of it connected’); B1’s problem lines are the site’s block promises turned around (see the open questions). EXCLUDED, from Wicflow’s current hero: stock footage of real work (a forklift, a tractor), a headline over full-bleed video, a linear drift over stock, and the cycling word. Also out: generic AI imagery, cartoon icons, bounce, invented names or figures, and the site’s other example companies. Loop: 37.60 ≡ 0.00 (frame 2256 = frame 0).

Open questions

For Karl and Wicflow before the animatic.

  1. B1’s VO, ‘Quotes go quiet. Enquiries pile up. Hours get typed in twice.’, turns the site’s block promises around (‘No open quote goes quiet’, ‘Every enquiry answered the same day by the right person’, ‘Hours from the sites go straight into invoices’). Is Wicflow happy to say the problems this plainly, or should B1’s VO be the softer ‘Every company loses time somewhere.’ (the pictures stay the same)?
  2. Hero videos usually autoplay muted. v2 tells the story in pictures, and the VO carries the words, so a muted visitor gets the pictures and the three text moments. Should the hero carry optional subtitles (a captions track, or small burned-in lines) for muted autoplay, or is the picture story enough?
  3. With the text column gone, the film can play full-bleed behind the hero headline ‘Built for the work that keeps your company on schedule.’ in place of the stock video, or as its own player beside it. Which? Full-bleed would need the subject kept clear of the headline’s side.
  4. Should the end button be ‘Book an AI mapping’ (as boarded: the story’s next step and the site’s main button) or the hero’s own ‘Get your free AI roadmap’?
  5. ‘Hosted in Finland’ and ‘One contact at Wicflow’ have left the film, because they have no literal picture. Keep them out, or add ‘…hosted in Finland’ back to B8’s VO without a visual?
  6. The return bars and time columns are schematic (no figures) and follow the site’s 01–05 order, so they imply that this is Kallio Lämpö’s payback order. Acceptable, or should every bar except the first be the same length?
Where the styleframes differ from the drawing specs
  • B1 1.20: the quiet quote’s activity trace hangs just below the card, so it reads at hero size; in the film it can sit inside the card’s footer.
  • B7 25.00: the world eases left to ×0.9 to make room for the hours card. It is the only beat where the world leaves the centre, and it returns by 25.70.
  • B10 36.40: the cards are drawn opaque during the morph so the world behind does not show through them.
