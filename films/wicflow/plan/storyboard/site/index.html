The work, in blocks

A company’s work folds into Wicflow’s wireframe block world, and off-the-shelf software won’t sit on it. The AI mapping lifts that work onto the site’s own Week 1 → Week 2 timeline, finds one more process and turns it into a costed plan ranked by return. The plan then lies down as the ground, and its top row rises as the periwinkle first block, guaranteed. Inside that block no open quote goes quiet. Block by block the system builds and lights up as one. For the CTA, the first block’s face seats into the wicflow mark, and then everything unbuilds back into the work so the film loops.

Block rules

Orthographic 2:1 isometric. A lattice point (i,j,h) sits on screen at (1360 + 120(i−j), 554 + 69.5(i+j) − 128h). A block in cell (c,r,k) spans c..c+1, r..r+1 and k..k+1. Its top face is a 240×139 rhombus, its height 128 px, and its stud an 88×51 ellipse, 20 px tall, on the top-face centre. Draw back to front (by c+r, then by k). There are 14 blocks: all nine base cells, level 1 at (0,0), (0,1), (1,0) and (2,0), and level 2 at (0,0). The silhouette spans x 1000–1720, y 170–971 (74% of the frame height). The named blocks are the site's Kallio Lämpö blocks (top-face centre in brackets): 01 Quote follow-ups (0,2,0) (1120,634.5), the first block; 02 Enquiries answered (2,2,0) (1360,773.5); 03 New customers (2,0,1) (1600,506.5); 04 Hours to invoices (0,1,1) (1240,437); 05 Owner's weekly report (1,0,1) (1480,437); 06 One system (0,0,2) (1360,239.5). States follow the story and are never skipped. WIRE: no fill, 1.5 px dashed rgba(223,230,243,.42), dash 6/6, marching at 12 px/s. GLASS: faces rgba(255,255,255,.06/.035/.05) top/left/right, 1.25 px edges rgba(255,255,255,.22). SOLID: top #F2F3FC, left #DCDFF5, right #C2C7EC, stud top #FFFFFF, 40 px glow rgba(185,193,255,.22), and its number on the left face in Fragment Mono 34 px #0B1220 at 35%. The first block alone goes through GLASS-PERI (faces rgba(185,193,255,.24/.14/.10), 1.5 px #B9C1FF edges, 24 px glow rgba(97,114,243,.35)) to PERI (top #CDD3FF, left #B9C1FF, right #98A2F5, stud top #DDE1FF, 60 px glow rgba(97,114,243,.45)). The card-to-block mapping holds for the whole film: QUOTE → 01, ENQUIRY → 02, HOURS → 04, REPORT → 05. New customers (03) is the block the mapping finds. Only block 01 is ever a periwinkle block, and 06 stays dashed until it connects, as in reference 3.

Type and layout

Every headline is Stack Sans Headline 500 at 96 px, tracking −2%, 100 px line pitch, at most about 15 characters a line. Support lines are Inter 44 px in the muted colour, 56 px pitch. Kickers, chips and leader labels are Fragment Mono 40 px; 28–36 px is used only for tags, column heads and the made-up-example footnote, none of which the story depends on. The text column is left-aligned at x 170–950, with the block world at x 1000–1720. The kinetic beat (B3) and the plan (B4) are centred, and the automation panel takes the cluster's place. Nothing essential sits outside x 96–1824, y 54–1026. Every line holds for at least 1.2 s after it lands; this is checked in each beat's timings. Before the Finnish version, check that Stack Sans Headline and Fragment Mono have ä and ö. Finnish runs 25–35% longer, so reflow to three lines or drop to 84 px, never below 80 px.

Motion identity

calm, clean, Apple. Arrivals decelerate (B3 soft start with a long settle; A5 rise slowed to 0.4 s; ARRIVE cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)). Exits accelerate (ease-in, 0.2–0.3 s). Landings end in a 2–4 px drift with no overshoot (B4 spring with damping 200; B9 follow-through 3/6 frames). No bounce, elastic or anticipation, and no camera pans. The only scale moves are the two zoom-throughs of block 01 (B16, 16 frames each way, cut on the fastest frame) and one breath out to 0.96 in the CTA. One movement leads at a time, and a new event lands every 0.5–0.8 s. Holds stay alive: the dashes march, the stack breathes 0–5 px, glows breathe and pulses run. Motion norm: the brief names no target, so plan for the house 7% median (motion_check.py at 60 fps). Thin wire lines barely register, so test B1–B2 first at the animatic stage. Sound: effects lead over a quiet music bed (louder if there is no VO), from films/alumni/sound/lib. Cues: felt clicks as blocks land; a dull thud and a soft 'wrong' tick on the slab; line-draw tones and ticks on the timeline; a card-shuffle swish on the re-rank; a deep whoosh and a plastic thock on the tilt and extrusion; typing ticks and a soft ding for each 'Draft ready'; rising clicks block by block; an arpeggio as the pulse lights each stud; a resolving chord under the CTA; a reverse shimmer into the loop.

Truth, exclusions and loop

Every on-screen phrase comes from wicflow.com. Used as written: 'Software made to work for every company never quite fits yours'; 'Custom automation and AI, not software off the shelf'; 'AI mapping', '1–2 weeks', 'fixed price', 'First we map where the time goes', 'Week 1' and 'Week 2'; 'costed plan', with 'what to build first, what it returns, what it costs' as the columns 'Build first · Returns · Costs'; 'Highest return first'; the five Kallio Lämpö block names and the 'First block' tag; 'First block · guaranteed'; 'No open quote goes quiet', 'Drafts ready for the salesperson'; 'Every block priced in writing before we start'; 'One system', 'All of it connected', 'in your name and colours', 'Hosted in Finland', 'One contact at Wicflow'; 'Let's find the first thing to build', 'Book an AI mapping', '300+ projects built'. Lightly shortened: 'Built around how you work' (from '…around how you actually work') and 'If it doesn't do what we agreed, you don't pay' (from '…you don't pay for it'). The only figures on screen are 1–2 weeks and 300+. The bars carry no digits, the quote rows use placeholder lines, and Kallio Lämpö is labelled as the site's made-up example. EXCLUDED, from Wicflow's current hero: stock footage of real work (a forklift, a tractor) and any other footage, a headline over full-bleed video, a linear drift over stock, and the headline's cycling word. Also out: generic AI imagery, cartoon icons, bounce, and the site's other example companies. Loop: 37.60 ≡ 0.00 (frame 2256 = frame 0). The card drift runs on a 3.76 s period (ten periods per loop), so pose and velocity match at the loop point, and no dashes are on screen at frame 0.

Where the styleframes differ from the drawing specs
  • B9 wordmark: set as the site nav actually sets it (site.css .brand: Manrope 800, −4% tracking), at 100 px beside the 156×100 mark, instead of Stack Sans Headline 600 at 68 px.
  • B3 chips: Inter 500 at 40 px (drawn as 36 px) so every story word stays at 40 px or more. The row is laid out from the measured words, centred on x 960 (26 px padding, 16 px gaps), so chip x positions and the scan point differ a few px from the drawn numbers; the B3 → B4 text growth becomes 40 → 44 px.
  • B6 pills: ‘Draft ready’ widens to x 1436–1690 and ‘Open’ sits at x 1530–1690; the header ‘First block’ pill is 214 px wide so the mono text fits; ‘Running’ is 40 px.
  • B2 slab: ‘OFF THE SHELF’ is printed in light grey (rgba(225,230,240,.62)); #0B1220 at 45% disappeared on the translucent grey slab over navy.
  • WIRE blocks carry a faint navy face tint so hidden edges read faintly through, as in reference 1. SOLID and PERI blocks are drawn above glass and wire, so the first block’s faces read fully through the glass in front, as in references 2–4.
  • Printed block numbers sit left of the face centre so the B8 light lines, which climb the faces at the centre, never cross them; the studs of solid blocks are drawn over the light lines so each line plugs into its stud.
  • Drop-throughs at 11.6 and 13.35 are drawn a little further along than specified (the new text nearer its baseline) so the frame stays legible; the lifted rows at 13.35 sit on opaque glass plates that hide the sinking row.
