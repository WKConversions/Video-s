## The business (facts from wicflow.com: only these on screen)
- **Who they are:** Wicflow builds custom automation and AI for Finnish companies: "AI that grows revenue for Finnish
  companies". Hero line: "Built for the work that keeps your company on schedule." Belief: software made to work for
  every company never quite fits yours; they build around how you actually work.
- **Approach:** "Built in blocks, highest return first."
  - They find where the company loses the most time and money.
  - They automate it in the order that pays back most: custom automation and AI, not off-the-shelf software.
- **Steps:**
  1. Learn how your company works: follow the real work.
  2. Find the work with the highest return: the AI mapping, a costed plan; the block that pays back most becomes the
     first block.
  3. Build it block by block: every block priced in writing before they start; if the first one doesn't do what was
     agreed, you don't pay for it.
  4. Run it and keep improving it: hosted in Finland, one contact at Wicflow.
- **AI mapping:** 1–2 weeks, fixed price, ends in a costed plan (what to build first, what it returns, what it costs).
  Its price comes off the first build.
- **Free AI roadmap:** about 2 minutes, checked by their team, full roadmap as a PDF within two working days, a 30-60-90
  day plan.
- **Proof:**
  - 300+ projects built; 30+ companies have worked with them;
  - €300M+ combined value of the organisations they work with;
  - 10+ teams helped to start using AI;
  - 3× the qualified leads in three months; 30 h a week freed from research.
- **Example blocks** for a made-up heating company (Kallio Lämpö, 12 people):
  - quote follow-ups (first block), enquiries answered, new customers, hours to invoices, owner's weekly report;
  - one system: "All of it connected, in your name and colours".
- **Other examples:** a machinery dealer (listings from photos, buyers for each machine…), an accounting firm (receipts
  sorted…).
- **CTAs:** "Book an AI mapping", "Get your free AI roadmap".

## The storyboard to deliver
- **Format:** a hero-section SaaS explainer, 16:9 1920×1080, about 30–40 s, 60 fps.
  - It must work muted: autoplay in the hero, with on-screen text carrying the story.
  - Write an optional English voice-over script line for each beat (British/neutral English; Karl can localise to
    Finnish later).
  - Make the ending loopable.
- **Style:** premium dark SaaS. The 3D isometric block world from the reference is the thread, "here and there": the
  company's work becomes blocks, the first block lights up, blocks connect into one system. Alternate it with flat UI
  beats (the mapping, a costed plan, a running automation) and kinetic type.
- **Motion:** Karl's house rules.
  - Constant calm motion; objects carry it with small settles (no cartoon bounce); smooth, never linear.
  - One element becomes the next scene.
  - Readable (each line ≥ 1.2 s).
  - A memorable flourish or two.
- **Truth:** only the facts above. The example company is the site's own made-up Kallio Lämpö.
