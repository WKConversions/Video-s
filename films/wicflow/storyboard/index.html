AI that moves your business forward.

A bright, spiced-up take on the concept of the Cartesian and ClearScaler films: one isometric map of your business and its market, in Wicflow's own white, ink and service tints. The site's live-AI blue dot leaves the assistant's chat box, runs the system Wicflow builds, finds Anna's company, writes, follows up, updates the CRM, connects the tools your team uses, runs one pilot, scales it, and comes home. The business grows a floor for each tint, and the dot becomes the full stop of wicflow.com.

Art directionKarl's references set the concept (one isometric world, a status chip top left, captions bottom left, paths travelling the world, a scale-out, a calm logo end). The composition is our own: a loop of track round the business and five stations (market, CRM, tools, team, desk).
Brandwicflow.com measured: white paper, ink #17171B, the three service tints (blue, sage, sand) as station plates and pale washes, the live-AI blue #1769C2 only for the AI. Space Grotesk and Manrope. The traced W mark.
MotionPrecise and energetic: expo-out arrivals, A1 whips for the dot between stations, A2 drop-throughs for the caption slots, small pops (8% or less) only on pills and badges. The camera only breathes. Final render with 8-sample motion blur.

Open questions

  • The full stop of wicflow.com arrives as the AI's blue dot and eases to ink for the hold. Keep it blue as a brand moment, or ink as the site's wordmark is?
  • The W mark is traced from the site's 384 px PNG. Ask Wicflow for the original vector before publishing.
  • The CRM record pairs two of the site's illustrative statuses (New contact → Interested prospect). Wicflow may prefer one verbatim row.
  • Music: 'It's Love' (Mixkit) is the pick. 'Motivating Mornings' (punchier) and an original composed bed are fitted too (sound/music, sound/bed_composed.wav) if Karl's ear prefers one.
  • The site shows the 15 tool logos the same way; check that Wicflow is fine with them in a film.
