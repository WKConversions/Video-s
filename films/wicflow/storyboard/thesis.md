# Thesis

Wicflow puts AI to work across your whole business, shown on one bright isometric map of your business and its market:
the site's live-AI blue dot leaves the assistant's chat box, moves your business forward, runs the system Wicflow builds,
finds the right company, writes to Anna, follows up, updates the CRM, connects the tools your team already uses, runs one
pilot, scales it across the map, and comes home; your business grows a floor for each of the site's three service tints.

Allowed patterns:
- The map: white paper, a loose isometric world (white blocks with hairline tops, tinted sides, soft shadows), the business
  as the one ink block (the site's black pill / dark well language). Concept from Karl's references (Cartesian, ClearScaler),
  composition our own (a loop of track plates round the business, five stations: market, CRM cabinet, tool plot, team, desk).
- The site's three service tints (blue = customer acquisition, sage = sales automation, sand = team/everyday work) as ground
  fields that grow out of their station, and as pale paper washes that grow out of an object and recede back into it (blue out
  of the market P9–P12, sage out of the loop junction P13–P14, sand out of the desk P18). One full live-AI-blue #1769C2 field,
  once, on "more customers", out of the farthest booked company and back into the business's roof.
- The live-AI blue #1769C2 is only for the AI: the dot (with the site's 12% halo), its routes and links, checks, the key words.
- The site's own demo (home page "AI at work" card): Building firm · 40 staff · Tampere; "Hi Anna, I saw you're hiring two
  salespeople…"; Your team approved it · Sent after a quick check; Anna replied "Sounds useful. Thursday at 10?"; Meeting
  booked · Thursday 10:00, in your calendar; times 09:02–11:41; the counter "Meetings booked this week" 7 → 8 (illustrative,
  as on the site). The chip says "Example workflow" whenever demo data shows.
- The CRM row: two of the site's illustrative sales-view statuses, "New contact" → "Interested prospect" (a synthesis of the
  site's example rows, named here). "Updated automatically" describes the site's "keep your CRM updated".
- "Repeated work: Research → Prepare a follow-up → Update your CRM" is the services page's own chain.
- The 15 tool logos exactly as the site's "Connected to your existing workflows" grid, no names, ≥44–56 px.
- No customer logos, no invented numbers (the 30+/10+/5 figures are not used), no people's photos.
- "How can I help?" is generic assistant UI copy (not a claim). "Your team" / "Your business" / "Pilot" are labels.
- The end card: the traced W mark, the wordmark "wicflow.com", the site's line "An AI partner that runs your sales and
  marketing." and its black "AI analysis" pill.
