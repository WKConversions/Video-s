# Production plan: Wicflow, "AI at work" (37.5 s, voice-over, music, effects)

## Project direction
- PURPOSE: show what Wicflow does (practical AI systems for sales, marketing and everyday work), for the site hero/about and LinkedIn.
- AUDIENCE: owners and sales/marketing leads of growing companies (Nordics), not technical.
- FORMAT: 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 37.5 s (1125 frames). Voice-over: ElevenLabs "Christina – Energetic Commercial Female" (vo/vo.mp3,
  35.0 s, force-aligned: vo/align.txt, vo/words.json). Music + effects from sound/cues.json.
- CORE MESSAGE: AI that doesn't just assist but moves the business: built systems that find prospects, write, follow up, update the
  CRM, on the tools you use; one pilot, measured, scaled; growth that lasts.
- TONE: energetic and optimistic, with Wicflow's calm practical clarity. Timing row between "confident" and "energetic": expo-out
  arrivals, A1 whips for the dot between stations, small (≤8%) overshoot only on badges/pills/bubbles, the camera only breathes.
- REFERENCES (concept, not layout): Cartesian hero, our ClearScaler film (Karl: "kinda the same concept but bright… spice it up").
- STYLE AND BRAND: wicflow.com (brand.json): paper #FFFFFF; ink #17171B / #0A0A0D; muted #606069; line #D6D6D9; live-AI blue #1769C2
  (+12% halo); tints blue #F1F5FB/#E6EDF7/#DFE7F3, sage #F1F6F1/#E5EEE6/#DDE8DE, sand #F8F4EC/#F0E9DC/#EAE1D1; Space Grotesk 600
  (−0.045em) for captions and the wordmark, Manrope for UI; radius 24 cards, pills; the traced W mark.
- ART DIRECTION: one bright isometric map (fixed 45° view, scale 1.08, a 1.6° drift over the film): the business (ink block) in a loop of
  16 white track plates; stations: the market (9 companies, back), the CRM cabinet (sage, back right), the tool plot (15 tiles with
  screen-facing logo badges, right), the team (three ink figures) and the desk with its paper stack (left). Screen-facing cards grow
  out of the objects they belong to (message out of the roof, reply out of Anna's company, record out of the CRM drawer, counter out of
  the pilot plot, "Repeated work" out of the desk). The site's "AI at work" chip top left. Captions bottom left.
- MOTION IDENTITY: precise-energetic. Signature curve expo-out (.16,1,.3,1) for ~80% of arrivals; DEPART (.55,0,.9,.4) for exits;
  MOVE/long-S for the dot's travels; A1 whip (whipIn/whipOut, cut on the fastest frame) for the dot between stations; A2 drop-through
  for caption slots and chip rows; B13 stepper for plates, sheets, tool rows; B11 for routes and links. Durations: quick 6, standard
  12, slow 24 frames. Entrance pattern: out of the ground (blocks, plates, tiles, floors) or out of the object a card belongs to.
- CAMERA: one slow breath for the whole film (kinetic breathe), settling before the end card; no pans or zooms. The only scale change
  is the business growing as an element (1 → 1.3 with its floors).
- TRANSITION FAMILY: (1) out of an object (cards hinge out of roof/drawer/desk, bubbles out of blocks, tints and washes out of stations,
  the blue field out of a booked company and back into the roof, the wordmark out of the dot); (2) object becomes the next thing (bubble,
  message, reply, sheets fold into the dot; bubbles flip into "Meeting booked"; the tints become the floors; the dot becomes the "."); (3)
  before/after on the same objects (dashed spurs turn solid, the world dims to one pilot and relights as its copies, slots roll).

## Colour plan
White paper all film. Ground fields: sage (CRM) from P6, blue (market) from P7, sand (team) from P8, converging under the business on P21
and becoming its floors on P22. Washes (pale tints over the paper, behind the world): blue 10.75–17.5 (market), sage 17.95–21.3 (junction),
sand 26.1–29.5 (desk). One full live-AI-blue field 29.46–31.0. End card: the OG card's pale-blue circle (#E3E9F4) behind the lockup.

## Sound plan

## Rules for the build
- Captions: Space Grotesk 600, 66 px, −0.045em, bottom left at x 110, lines at y 852 and 934; words blur-rise on their spoken words; the
  key word in #1769C2 with an underline or strike where it acts; lists are one slot that rolls (A2) on the spoken item.
- Chip: the site's AI-at-work card, top left (72, 64): a 300 px pill, growing to 660×214 for the demo rows (30 px titles, 23 px subs);
  folds back to the pill between beats; "Example workflow" whenever demo data shows.
- Cards: white, radius 24, the site's float shadow and hairline; text 34–42 px; every card grows out of its object and folds back into it.
- Nothing bounces except badges, pills and bubbles (≤8%); the camera only breathes; one lead motion at a time.
