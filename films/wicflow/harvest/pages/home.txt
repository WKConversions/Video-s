Common questions.
Do I need to change the way I work?
We build around the tools and workflows you already use. We agree on any changes or integration limits before we start.
Will this take more of my time?
We keep your time commitment small. We agree on the goal and scope together, then Wicflow handles the build and setup. You provide the necessary access and review the result with us.
Do you offer a guarantee?
Every project starts with a clear scope and agreed deliverables. Depending on the project, payment can also be tied to performance. We agree on the success criteria and commercial terms before you commit.
How quickly can we see results?
We start with a focused pilot that can make a useful difference quickly. We usually aim for the first results within a few days to three weeks, depending on what we build and the access we need. We agree on the timeline before starting.
What if I don’t know where to start?
You don’t need a finished idea. Show us where sales slow down or repeated work takes up your team’s time. We’ll help you choose one useful pilot with a clear goal. You can also try our free AI roadmap before we talk.
Get your AI roadmap
Does my team need technical skills?
No. Your team brings knowledge of your customers and how the work gets done. Wicflow handles the technical setup and shows the people involved how to use what we build.
How much does it cost?
The price depends on what we build and run for you. We agree on the scope, pricing and any software costs before starting. Where performance-based pricing fits, we also agree on the result that triggers payment.
How will we know if it is working?
We agree on a starting point and a measurable goal before the pilot. Depending on the work, that could mean more qualified sales conversations, better conversion or less time spent on a repeated task. We review the results together before deciding what to expand.
What happens after the first pilot?
We review what worked and decide on the next step together. That can mean handing the workflow over to your team, improving it or having Wicflow run and develop it through an ongoing partnership. We agree on ownership and support as part of the scope.
What access to our tools and data will you need?
That depends on the workflow. We agree on the information and permissions needed before connecting anything. We start with limited access and approved examples where possible, and clarify which tools will process your data.
What is your business model?
We want to build long-term partnerships with companies that are hungry for growth. We start with a focused pilot, prove what works and then build and run more of your sales and marketing together. Ongoing work has an agreed scope, and we use performance-based pricing where it fits the project.
What kinds of companies do you work with?
We work with companies that want more customers, stronger sales and a practical way to use AI. You can sell to businesses or consumers, and you don’t need to be in a particular industry. The best starting point is a real problem, a clear growth goal and someone on your team who can help us understand the work.
What AI tools and models do you use?
We use a range of AI tools and models. Most commonly, we use Codex from OpenAI and Claude from Anthropic to help build and improve solutions. For your workflows, we choose tools based on the task, your existing setup, data requirements and cost. You don’t need to switch to our internal tools.
Can AI automation comply with GDPR?
Yes. AI workflows can be designed to meet GDPR requirements. For each project, we need to check the purpose and legal basis for processing, the data involved, access, retention, supplier agreements and any international transfers. Compliance depends on both the setup and how it is used. We agree on these requirements with you and involve your data protection or legal adviser where needed.
Can you work alongside our current team or agency?
Yes. We can work alongside your sales team, marketing team and existing partners. We agree on who owns each part of the work, how information is shared and where Wicflow adds value.
Will AI send messages or make decisions without our approval?
We agree on the approval rules before anything goes live. A pilot can prepare drafts and recommendations for your team to review. If you want steps to run automatically, we define which actions are allowed, when a person needs to step in and how to pause the workflow.
What would you like to improve?
Talk it through with Felix. We’ll look at your sales and marketing and find a practical first step.
