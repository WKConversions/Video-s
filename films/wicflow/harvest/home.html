    How is this different from other AI offers?

    Most offers are a ready-made tool that your company has to adapt to. We work the other way round: we learn how you work, estimate what automating each process would return, and build the one that pays back most first. Nothing gets built that you don’t need.

    What does the AI mapping cost?

    A fixed price that we agree before we start, for one to two weeks of work with your team. If you go ahead, the whole price comes off the first build.

    What do we keep if we don’t continue?

    The mapping’s plan: what to build, in what order, what it would return and what it costs. From a build you keep what we built into your own tools, such as the lists, the email texts and the automations. Things that run on our platform, such as Company Brain, keep running only under a monthly agreement with us.

    What does it cost after the mapping?

    Each block is priced in writing before we start, and the first one is guaranteed: if it doesn’t do what we agreed, you don’t pay for it. Running what you keep has one monthly price. For reference, Company Brain starts at €1,900 a month. Prices exclude VAT (25.5%).

    Do we have to switch systems?

    No. What we build connects to what you already use, such as Pipedrive, HubSpot, Outlook, Gmail, Netvisor and Procountor.

    Does AI send anything without our approval?

    You approve the target group and the texts before a campaign starts. Replies to customers go out only once someone on your team has approved them. Company Brain never sends anything on its own.

    Where is our data?

    Stored in Finland: on servers in Helsinki, backups included. The AI runs in the EU, and under our agreements with the AI providers nothing you send is stored and your data is never used to train AI models.

    Does our team need technical skills?

    No. We set everything up, connect it to your tools and show the people who use it how it works. Your team brings what it knows about your customers.
