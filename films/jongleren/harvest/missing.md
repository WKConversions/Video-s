# What the film would want and the site doesn't have
- No screens of the app's step tracker (only the home screen): the step list in the app is rebuilt from the
  site's five steps and labelled with the site's own step names.
- No photo of an intake video call with a student: the intake card is drawn as UI (no invented people).
- No vector logo: the monogram is cut from the site's 1536 px PNG (the four juggling balls redrawn as vectors
  so they can move).
- The school's requirements are not specified anywhere: the checklist shows neutral lines, not invented rules.
