## The three audiences

Colour code on the site: graduates = green dot `#028B45`, employers = orange dot `#FE6C02`, agencies = lime dot `#6ABB24`.

| Audience label | Headline | Card copy (verbatim) | CTA | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "For graduates & former interns" | "Your career abroad" | "Finished your studies and ready for working life? Alumni connects graduates with internship experience to job opportunities in foreign labour markets." / "Tell us about your background, interests and where you’d like to work. Start a conversation about your next step." | "Explore my next step" | Hero strip (section-01) + green card (section-04) |
| "For employers" | "Your next colleague" | "Looking for people at the beginning of their careers? Connect with graduates who bring a qualification, internship experience and ambition to their next role." / "Tell us about your team, the roles you’re recruiting for and the experience you’re looking for." | "Discuss a hiring need" | Hero strip + orange-tint card (section-04) |
| "For recruitment agencies" | "Your next talent connection" | "Recruiting for international vacancies? Alumni brings recruitment agencies together with graduates entering the labour market." / "Let’s discuss the roles and markets you work with, and explore where there could be a fit." | "Explore a collaboration" | Hero strip + wide white card (section-04b) |

Note for the VO: the script says "businesses" and "recruitment partners"; the site says "employers" and "recruitment agencies". On-screen labels should use the site's terms ("For employers", "For recruitment agencies") unless the client says otherwise.
