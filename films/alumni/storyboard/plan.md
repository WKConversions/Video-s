# Alumni — "Your next chapter starts abroad" (website hero film, plan)

## 1 Brief
- **Client:** Alumni (small-salad-e287.raphael-0d6.workers.dev): connects graduates with internship
  experience to employers and recruitment agencies with opportunities abroad. Independent company by the
  people behind JongLeren and K.B.
- **Deliverable:** the website hero film, 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 38.5 s: the supplied ElevenLabs
  voice-over ("Christina – Energetic Commercial American Female", 36.75 s) plus a hold on the end card.
  English, as the site.
- **Audience:** graduates and former interns (first reader), employers and recruitment agencies (second).
- **Message:** your internship was a beginning; Alumni connects that experience with opportunities abroad,
  for graduates, businesses and recruitment partners alike.
- **Tone:** energetic, optimistic, modern; Karl asks for **the 5% motion norm** (a typical frame has about
  5% of its area changing, something moves in 90%+ of frames, no still over 0.5 s) and a **SaaS look**.
  Motion comes from content (windows drifting at different depths, cables drawing with travelling orbs,
  type building, photos pushing inside windows, the mesh drifting), never from camera swings (K.B lessons).
- **Where it plays:** a website hero, which usually autoplays muted: every line must read without sound.
- **Assumptions:** 16:9; the script exactly as written; music and effects copyright-free.

## 2 Look and material
Style: **minimal gradient SaaS** (library), its palette built from Alumni's brand, not the references'
blue-violet: the logo's orange `#FD6C01`, lime `#6ABC24` and green `#028C45`, the site's sage `#D8E4DA`,
off-white `#F7F8F4` and deep green text. Mesh: pale apricot, pale lime and mint in the corners, white where
the type sits, fixed grain. Key phrases filled with the logo's orange → green. The site's font (Lato) for
everything. Frosted-glass windows and tiles, skeleton lines where detail isn't needed, real words only where
they matter (the site's own). Photos only inside windows: the site's two city photos (a harbour town,
a hillside city by the water). The AL monogram rebuilt in layers (orange A, lime and green L).

## 5 Thesis and art direction
**Thesis:** *From experience to opportunity — the logo is the journey.* The orange A is the climb you've
made (study, internship); the green L is the turn into what comes next, abroad. The site draws its icons as
one line that runs orange into green. The film's thread is that line: it leaves the finished internship,
rises, turns, becomes the AL, runs as the cables that connect a graduate to employers and agencies, draws
the path forward and the flight to a new country, and ends between the words "experience" and
"opportunity", in the logo's own colours.

Allowed patterns: the orange → green line and cables with a travelling orb; frosted windows and tiles;
chips; initials avatars (no invented faces); the site's own photos inside windows; the site's words and
section names; the AL monogram. Not allowed: invented employers, agencies, vacancies, numbers or partner
logos; dark mode; icon per noun; 3D spins; bounce on big things; camera moves on transitions.

**Motion identity:** smooth SaaS energy. Arrivals: soft ease-out (`ARRIVE .22,1,.36,1`) with the style's
scale-and-fade for tiles; travel: `MOVE .65,0,.35,1`; exits `DEPART`; no bounce. Durations: quick 6–8 f,
standard 12–16 f, slow 22–28 f. Entrance pattern: out of the line (masks), or scale-and-fade from depth.
The camera only breathes; one slow push into the end card.
