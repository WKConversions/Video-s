Direction

PurposeA website and social explainer for VoxFlow, the AI receptionist for Dutch small businesses: every call answered, questions handled, appointments booked, while you keep working.
Format16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 32.0 s. Dutch voice-over (ElevenLabs, Vincent), force-aligned word by word; music and effects mixed to −15 LUFS.
Lookvoxflow.nl's own: warm cream, deep teal #0F6E6A, white cards with soft wide shadows, Inter 800 headlines with the key word in teal, Geist for the interface, the site's receptionist portrait, wordmark and photography; free-licensed footage of a practice and a caller.
MotionNo drifting layers. Things appear where they live, on their word; colour fields grow out of objects; the two scene changes that travel are whips that move both scenes on one curve. The camera never sways: one push per shot on what the line is about (at most about 5%/s), let go only while the whole frame changes. Median frame motion 4.1% on the draft.
ThreadOne call: it rings in a busy practice and is missed, the missed calls pile up, VoxFlow flips them to answered, the receptionist takes them, the caller asks for tomorrow, the appointment lands in the agenda, and an appointment finally becomes “Start gratis”.
SoundGenerated phone sounds (a soft ring, a missed call, a pick-up, a notification) and the WKConversions effects library, one sound per event and never on a key word; the bed ducks under the voice and dips 4 dB under the call to action.

Open questions

  • The lock screen shows VoxFlow notifications on the owner's phone (as in the approved storyboard). voxflow.nl mentions the dashboard summary, not push notifications: please confirm VoxFlow notifies the owner, or the phone shows only the call being taken.
